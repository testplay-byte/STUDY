Example 24: Sketch the graph of (a) $y = \sqrt{x}$ (b) $y = \sqrt{x - 2}$ (c) $y = \sqrt{x + 2}$

Which kind of shift did you observe after sketching the graphs.

Solution:

(a) $y = \sqrt{x}$(b) $y = \sqrt{x - 2}$(c) $y = \sqrt{x + 2}$

Above graphs show a horizontal shift. The graph of the function $y = \sqrt{x - 2}$ can be obtained by transforming the graph of given function 2 units right to the origin while the graph of $y = \sqrt{x + 2}$ can be obtained by transforming the graph of given function 2 units left to the origin.
