(vii)The function $f: R \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ defined by $f(x) = x^2 + 1$, is:

(a)onto but not on to one

(b)one to one but not onto

(c)neither one to one nor onto

(d)both one to one and onto

(a)$f$ is one to one

(b)$f$ is onto

(c)$f$ is both one to one and onto

(ix)The inverse function of $f(x) = x^3$, is:

(b)$f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt{x^{-3}}$

(a)one to one but not onto

(c)both one to one and onto

(d)neither one to one nor onto

(b)$f^{-1}(x) = -x$
