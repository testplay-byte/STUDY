Organized into six distinct sections, Basic Statistics Part-II provides you with:

Short Definitions: The first thing that makes this book stand out form rest of the books available in the market are short definitions. It is the distinction of this book that complex and confusing definitions of basic concepts have been gleaned in such a simple and effective way that even a man entirely unknown to Statistics can easily understand and learn them by heart. The definitions are brief yet comprehensive. It is claimed that these definitions cover almost the whole of the questions coming in examinations based on this section.

Multiple Choice Questions: The second characteristic that makes this book worth reading are the number of solved Multiple Choice Questions. The biggest distinction of these Multiple Choice Questions leis in there being able to cover almost every angle of not only the statement of the questions but also the wide variety of options written. Every angle has been kept in mind the paper, examinations, level of the students and requirement of the subject itself. These Multiple Choice Questions have been a montage of simplicity and effort. A part of Multiple Choice Questions has been solved in such a plain way that a single reading will enable students understand those Multiple Choice Questions. But in some Multiple Choice Questions, the students will have to put some effort in order to understand and analyses their answers.

Short Questions: The third trait that this book celebrates are the short questions. These short questions not only cover the complete range of possible short questions occurring in the examinations but also let the students relish the certainty of these questions to definitely come the examinations.
