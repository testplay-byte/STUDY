# Page 16 — Unit 02: Limit, Continuity and Derivative

> 📄 Original scan: [0016.jpg](../../../Raw/Mathematics/Unit-02-Limit-Continuity-and-Derivative/0016.jpg) · printed page 58

**Example 15:** Find: $\frac{d}{dx}[x^4] = 4x^3$, $\frac{d}{dx}[x^7] = 7x^6$, $\frac{d}{dx}[x^{50}] = 50x^{49}$, $\frac{d}{dx}[x^{200}] = 200x^{199}$,

$$\frac{d}{dx}[x^{31}] = 31x^{30}$$

We can apply this formula for all real numbers like:

$$\begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}\left[\sqrt{x}\right] &= \frac{d}{dx}\left[x^{\frac{1}{2}}\right] = \frac{1}{2}x^{\frac{1}{2}-1} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[x^{\frac{4}{5}}\right] &= \frac{4}{5}x^{\frac{4}{5}-1} = \frac{4}{5x^{\frac{1}{5}}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{1}{x}\right] &= \frac{d}{dx}x^{-1} = -\frac{1}{x^{2}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{1}{x^{50}}\right] &= \frac{d}{dx}x^{-50} = -\frac{50}{x^{51}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[10x^{\frac{1}{3}}\right] &= 10\frac{d}{dx}x^{\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{10x^{-\frac{2}{3}}}{3} = \frac{10}{3x^{\frac{2}{3}}} \end{aligned}$$

> **Derivative of constant function:**
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx}[c]=\frac{d}{dx}[cx^0]=c\frac{d}{dx}[x^0]=c.0x^{0-1}=0$$
>
> $\frac{d}{dx}[c] = 0$ like $\frac{d}{dx}[10] = 0$

> **Theorem:**  
> If $n$ is any real number
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx} x^n = nx^{n-1}$$

> **Theorem:**  
> If $c$ is any real number
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx} [cf(x)] = cf'(x)$$

> **Sum and Difference Rule:**  
> If $f$ and $g$ are differentiable function, then
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx} [f(x) + g(x)] = f'(x) + g'(x)$$
>
> In words, the derivative of a sum equals to the sum of the derivatives and the derivative of difference is equal to the difference of the derivatives.

**Example 16:**

i. $\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx} [2x^6 + x^{-9}]$

$$\begin{aligned} &= \frac{d}{dx} [2x^6] + \frac{d}{dx} [x^{-9}] \\ &= 2.\frac{d}{dx} [x^6] + (-9)x^{-9-1} \\ &= 2(6)x^5 - 9x^{-10} \\ &= 12x^5 - \frac{9}{x^{10}} \end{aligned}$$

ii. $\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx} \left[4x^5 - \frac{1}{2}x^4 + 9x^3 + 7\right]$

$$\begin{aligned} &= 4\frac{d}{dx}[x^5] - \frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dx}[x^4] + 9\frac{d}{dx}[x^3] + \frac{d}{dx}[7] \\ &= 4(5)x^4 - \frac{1}{2}(4)x^3 + 9(3)x^2 + 0 \\ &= 20x^4 - 2x^3 + 27x^2 \end{aligned}$$

iii. $\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx} \left[5x^{-1} - \frac{1}{5}x\right]$

$$\begin{aligned} &= 5\frac{d}{dx}[x^{-1}] - \frac{1}{5}\frac{d}{dx}[x] \\ &= -5x^{-2} - \frac{1}{5}x^{1-1} = \frac{-5}{x^{2}} - \frac{1}{5} \end{aligned}$$
