**Example 25:**

Differentiate $y = \sin^{-1} 5x$ w.r.t. $x$.

**Solution:** $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx} \sin^{-1} 5x$

$$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - (5x)^2}} \cdot \frac{d}{dx} 5x$$

$$\frac{d}{dx} \sin^{-1} 5x = \frac{5}{\sqrt{1 - 25x^2}}$$

---

**Example 26:** Differentiate $y = \tan^{-1} 12x$

**Solution:** $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx} \tan^{-1} 12x$

$$= \frac{1}{1 + (12x)^2} \cdot \frac{d}{dx} 12x$$

$$\frac{d}{dx} \tan^{-1} 12x = \frac{12}{1 + 144x^2}$$

**Example 27:** Differentiate $y = \sec^{-1} x^2$

**Solution:** $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx} \sec^{-1} x^2$

$$= \frac{1}{x^2 \sqrt{(x^2)^2 - 1}} \cdot \frac{d}{dx} x^2$$

$$= \frac{1}{x^2 \sqrt{(x^2)^2 - 1}} \cdot (2x)$$

$$\frac{d}{dx} \sec t x^2 = \frac{2x}{x^2 \sqrt{x^4 - 1}}$$
