**Example 41:** Find the first five derivatives of $f(x) = 2x^4 - 6x^3 + 7x^2 + 5x - 10$ w.r.t. $x$.

**Solution:** We have

$$\begin{aligned}
f'(x) &= 8x^3 - 18x^2 + 14x + 5 \\
f''(x) &= 24x^2 - 36x + 14 \\
f'''(x) &= 48x - 36 \\
f^{(4)}(x) &= 48 \\
f^{(5)}(x) &= 0
\end{aligned}$$

**Example 42:** Find the third derivatives of $y = \frac{1}{x^3}$

**Solution:** We have $y = \frac{1}{x^3} = x^{-3}$

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{dy}{dx} &= -3x^{-4} \\
\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} &= (-3)(-4)x^{-5} = 12x^{-5} \\
\frac{d^3y}{dx^3} &= (12)(-5)x^{-6} = -60x^{-6} = \frac{-60}{x^6}
\end{aligned}$$
