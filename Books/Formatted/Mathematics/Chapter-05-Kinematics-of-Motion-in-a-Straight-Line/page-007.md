6. Ahmed leaves home at $11\text{ am}$. He cycles at a speed of $16\text{ km/h}$ for $90\text{ minutes}$. He stops for half an hour. Ahmed then cycles home and arrives at $3\text{ pm}$.
(i) Draw a displacement-time graph to show Ahmed's journey.

7. Dabeer leaves at 14:00. He drives at an average speed of $14\text{ km/h}$ for $3\frac{1}{2}$ hours.

Draw a displacement-time graph to show Dabeer's journey.
