(ix) A heavy object is released near the surface of earth and falls freely. Air resistance can be ignored. Which statement about the acceleration of the object due to gravity is correct?

(a) The acceleration depends on mass of the object.
(b) The acceleration depends on volume of the object.
(c) The acceleration is constant.
(d) The acceleration is initially zero and increases as the object falls.

(v) What is average speed of the car for whole journey?
(vi) What is the average speed of the car for the time it was moving?
