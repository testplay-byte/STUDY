# Page 8 — Unit 06: Analytical Geometry

> 📄 Original scan: [0008.jpg](../../../Raw/Mathematics/Unit-06-Analytical-Geometry/0008.jpg) · printed page 167

   Slope of $\text{OA} = \frac{0 - 0}{6 - 0} = 0$,
   Slope of $\text{OB} = \frac{6 - 0}{0 - 0} = \infty$ (undefined)

Slope of $\text{AB} = \frac{6 - 0}{0 - 6} = -1$

Now, we find slopes of right bisectors.

Slope of right bisector $\text{CD} = \frac{-1}{\text{Slope of OA}} = \frac{-1}{0} = \infty$

Slope of right bisector $\text{EF} = \frac{-1}{\text{Slope of OB}} = \frac{-1}{\infty} = 0$

Slope of right bisector $\text{GH} = \frac{-1}{\text{Slope of AB}} = \frac{-1}{-1} = 1$

Equation of right bisector CD is:

$$y - 0 = \infty (x - 3) \quad \Rightarrow \quad x - 3 = 0 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (1)$$

Equation of right bisector EF is:

$$y - 3 = 0 (x - 0) \quad \Rightarrow \quad y - 3 = 0 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (2)$$

Equation of right bisector GH is:

$$y - 3 = 1 (x - 3) \quad \Rightarrow \quad y - x = 0 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (3)$$

Equations (1), (2) and (3) are equations of right bisectors of sides of triangle ABC.

Solving (1) and (2), we see that: $x = 3, y = 3$

$\therefore$ Circumcenter = G(3, 3) ............ (A)

(ii) Centroid is the point of intersection of three medians of a triangle.

First of all, we find the mid points of three sides.

Mid-point of OA = C(3, 0)

Mid-point of OB = D(0, 3)

Mid-point of AB = E(3, 3)

[Figure F1]

Equation of median OE is:

$$\frac{y - 0}{3 - 0} = \frac{x - 0}{3 - 0} \quad \text{(two-point formula)}$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad y = x \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (4)$$

Equation of median AD is:

$$\frac{y - 0}{3 - 0} = \frac{x - 6}{0 - 6} \quad \text{(two-point formula)}$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad -6y = 3x - 18 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 3x + 6y - 18 = 0$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad x + 2y - 6 = 0 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (5)$$

> **Key Facts**
>
> *   Medians of a triangle trisect each other (i.e., bisect each other in the ratio 2 : 1)
Equation of median BC is:

$$\frac{y - 6}{0 - 6} = \frac{x - 0}{3 - 0} \quad \text{(two-point formula)}$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad 3y - 18 = -6x \quad \Rightarrow \quad 6x + 3y - 18 = 0$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad 2x + y - 6 = 0 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (6)$$

Solving (4) and (5), we see that: $x = 2, y = 2$

$\therefore$ Centroid = P(2, 2) ............ (B)
