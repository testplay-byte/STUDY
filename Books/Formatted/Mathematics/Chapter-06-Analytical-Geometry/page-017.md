**Special cases:**

(i) When $\theta = 0$, then $\tan \theta = 0$ and equation (3) implies $h^2 - ab = 0$ or $h^2 = ab$ which is the condition for the lines to be coincident.

(ii) When $\theta = 90^\circ$, then $\tan \theta = \text{undefined}$ and equation (3) implies $a + b = 0$ which is condition for the lines to be orthogonal. i.e., sum of coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ is 0.

**Example 6:**

Find measure of acute angle between lines represented by $6x^2 - xy - y^2 = 0$.

**Solution:**

Given equation is $6x^2 - xy - y^2 = 0$

Here, $\quad a=6, \quad h=\frac{-1}{2}, \quad b=-1$

If $\theta$ is the measure of acute angle between lines, then:

$$\tan \theta = \frac{2\sqrt{h^2-ab}}{a+b} = \frac{2\sqrt{\frac{1}{4}+6}}{6-1} = \frac{5}{5} = 1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \theta = \tan^{-1}(1) = 45^\circ$$

**Example 7:**

Find a joint equation of straight lines through the origin and perpendicular to the lines represented by $3x^2 + 5xy + 2y^2 = 0$

> **Key Facts**
>
> *   If $y = mx \ldots\ldots\ldots (i)$ is equation of line passing through origin, then the line perpendicular to (i) through origin is $y = -\frac{1}{m}x$.

**Solution:**

$$3x^2 + 5xy + 2y^2 = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 3x^2 + 3xy + 2xy + 2y^2 = 0$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad 3x(x+y) + 2y(x+y)=0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad (x+y)(3x+2y)=0$$

Thus, the lines represented by the given equation are:

$$x+y = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad 3x+2y=0$$

$$\Rightarrow \qquad y = -x \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (1) \qquad \text{and} \qquad y = \frac{-3x}{2} \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots (2)$$

The line through $(0, 0)$ and perpendicular to (1) is:

$$y = x \qquad \text{or} \qquad y-x=0 \qquad \ldots\ldots\ldots (3)$$

The line through $(0, 0)$ and perpendicular to (2) is:

$$y = \frac{2x}{3} \qquad \text{or} \qquad 3y-2x=0 \qquad \ldots\ldots\ldots (4)$$

Joint equation of lines (3) and (4) is:

$$(y-x)(3y-2x)=0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 3y^2 - 2xy - 3xy + 2x^2 = 0$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad 3y^2 - 5xy + 2x^2 = 0. \quad \text{or} \quad 2x^2 - 5xy + 3y^2 = 0 .$$
