**Example 9:**

A triangular park is bounded by three straight roads that meet at points A(2, 3), B(8, 5) and C(5, 10). A city planner needs to determine the area of the park for landscaping purpose. How can he determine the area of the park?

**Solution:**

Here, $x_1=2$, $y_1=3$, $x_2=8$, $y_2=5$, $x_3=5$ and $y_3=10$

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Area of triangular park} &= \frac{1}{2} \times \begin{vmatrix} x_1 & y_1 & 1 \\ x_2 & y_2 & 1 \\ x_3 & y_3 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \frac{1}{2} \times \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 8 & 5 & 1 \\ 5 & 10 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \times [2(5 - 10) - 3(8 - 5) + 1(80 - 25)] \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \times [- 10 - 9 + 55] = \frac{1}{2} \times [36] = 18 \end{aligned}$$

Area of the plot = 18 square units

**Example 10:**

A railway engineer is designing an intersection where two rail tracks meet at a junction. The joint equation representing the tracks is given by $x^2 - 4xy - 12y^2 = 0$. Determine the angle between the individual lines representing the tracks.

**Solution:**

Given joint equation of the tracks is:

$$x^2 - 4xy - 12y^2 = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x^2 + 2xy - 6xy - 12y^2 = 0$$

$$\Rightarrow \quad x(x + 2y) - 6y(x + 2y) = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad (x + 2y)(x - 6y) = 0$$

The lines representing the tracks are:

$$x + 2y = 0 \qquad (i)$$
$$x - 6y = 0 \qquad (ii)$$

From (i) and (ii), we have:

$$y = -\frac{1}{2}x \qquad (iii)$$
$$y = \frac{1}{6}x \qquad (iv)$$

Slopes of lines (iii) and (iv) are:

$$m_1 = -\frac{1}{2} \quad \text{and} \quad m_2 = \frac{1}{6}$$
