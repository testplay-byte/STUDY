**Example 2:** Find the centre and radius of the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 10y + 18 = 0$.

**Solution:**

To find the centre and radius we convert the given equation of circle into its standard form

$$\begin{aligned}
&(x^2 - 6x) + (y^2 - 10y) + 18 = 0 \\
&(x^2 - 6x + 9 - 9) + (y^2 - 10y + 25 - 25) + 18 = 0 \\
&(x^2 - 6x + 9) + (y^2 - 10y + 25) - 9 - 25 + 18 = 0 \\
&(x - 3)^2 + (y - 5)^2 - 16 = 0 \Rightarrow (x - 3)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 16 \\
&(x - 3)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 4^2
\end{aligned}$$

Comparing it with $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$, we have:

$$\text{Centre} = (h, k) = (3, 5) \text{ and radius } = r = 4$$

## 7.2.3 Equation of a Circle with Different Conditions

**Example 3:** Find the equation of the circle passing through the points $(1, 2)$, $(2, 3)$ and $(3, 5)$.

**Solution:**

Consider a circle which passes through the points P(1, 2), Q(2, 3) and R(3, 5). Let C$(h, k)$ be the centre of the circle [Figure F1]

Then, $|PC| = |QC|$

$$\Rightarrow \sqrt{(h - 1)^2 + (k - 2)^2} = \sqrt{(h - 2)^2 + (k - 3)^2}$$

Squaring both sides:

$$(h - 1)^2 + (k - 2)^2 = (h - 2)^2 + (k - 3)^2$$

$$\Rightarrow h^2 - 2h + 1 + k^2 - 4k + 4 = h^2 - 4h + 4 + k^2 - 6k + 9$$

$$\Rightarrow 2h + 2k = 8$$

$$\Rightarrow h + k = 4 \quad \text{(i)}$$

Also $|PC| = |RC|$

$$\Rightarrow \sqrt{(h - 1)^2 + (k - 2)^2} = \sqrt{(h - 3)^2 + (k - 5)^2}$$

Squaring both sides:

$$(h - 1)^2 + (k - 2)^2 = (h - 3)^2 + (k - 5)^2$$

$$\Rightarrow h^2 - 2h + 1 + k^2 - 4k + 4 = h^2 - 6h + 9 + k^2 - 10k + 25$$

$$\Rightarrow 4h + 6k = 29 \quad \text{(ii)}$$

Multiplying (i) with 4 and subtracting (ii) from it, we get:

$$\begin{aligned}
&\Rightarrow 4h + 4k = 16 \\
&\underline{4h + 6k = 29} \\
&\qquad -2k = -13 \qquad \Rightarrow \quad k = \frac{13}{2}
\end{aligned}$$

Putting in (i), we get:

$$\Rightarrow h + \frac{13}{2} = 4 \Rightarrow h = 4 - \frac{13}{2} = -\frac{5}{2}$$

Thus, the centre of circle $= C \left( -\frac{5}{2}, \frac{13}{2} \right)$

The radius of circle is:

$$r = |CP| = \sqrt{\left(1 + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(2 - \frac{13}{2}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2 + \left(-\frac{9}{2}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{49}{4} + \frac{81}{4}} = \sqrt{\frac{130}{4}}$$
