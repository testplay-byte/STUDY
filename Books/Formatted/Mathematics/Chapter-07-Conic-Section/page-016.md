## 7.7.5 Elements of Parabola and its Graph

To find the elements of parabola when its equation is given; first we convert the given equation into the standard form and then compare it with one of the four standard equations and then find the elements of parabola.

**Example 12:** Find elements of parabola with equation $y = x^2 - 3x + 7$ and draw its graph.

**Solution:** Given equation of parabola is:

$$\begin{aligned} y &= x^2 - 3x + 7 \quad \Rightarrow x^2 - 3x = y - 7 \\ \Rightarrow x^2 - 3x + \frac{9}{4} &= y - 7 + \frac{9}{4} \quad \Rightarrow \left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = y - \frac{19}{4} \end{aligned}$$

Let $x - \frac{3}{2} = X$ and $y - \frac{19}{4} = Y$ then $X^2 = Y$ or $X^2 = 4\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)Y$

Which is of the form $X^2 = 4aY$ where $a = \frac{1}{4}$.

Now we write the elements of the parabola.

**Vertex:** We know that vertex is at $(0,0)$ i.e. $(X,Y) = (0,0)$
$\Rightarrow X = 0$ and $Y = 0$
$\Rightarrow x - \frac{3}{2} = 0$ and $y - \frac{19}{4} = 0$ $\Rightarrow x = \frac{3}{2}$ and $y = \frac{19}{4}$

Thus, the vertex of the given parabola is at $\left(\frac{3}{2}, \frac{19}{4}\right)$.

**Focus:** The focus of the parabola is at $(0,a)$. i.e., $(X,Y) = (0,a)$
$\Rightarrow X = 0$ and $Y = a$ $\Rightarrow x - \frac{3}{2} = 0$ and $y - \frac{19}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$ $\Rightarrow x = \frac{3}{2}$ and $y = 5$

Thus, the focus of the parabola is at $\left(\frac{3}{2}, 5\right)$.

**Axis of Parabola:** Equation of axis of parabola is $X = 0$.
$\Rightarrow x - \frac{3}{2} = 0$ or $x = \frac{3}{2}$

Which is the equation of axis of parabola.

**Directrix:** Equation of directrix of parabola is $Y = -a$ or $Y + a = 0$
$\Rightarrow y - \frac{19}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = 0$ $\Rightarrow y - \frac{9}{2} = 0$.

Which is the equation of directrix of parabola.

**Length of Latus Rectum:**

Length of latus rectum is $4a = 4\left(\frac{1}{4}\right) = 1$ unit

**Graph:** To draw the graph of parabola, we find its $x$-intercept and $y$-intercept. For $x$-intercept put $y=0$ in the equation $y = x^2 - 3x + 7$. We have:
$$x^2 - 2x + 7 = 0$$
