Thus, equation of tangent line at P is:

$$\begin{aligned} y - (-1) &= 1 \left( x - \frac{1}{4} \right) &\Rightarrow& &y + 1 &= x - \frac{1}{4} \\ \Rightarrow \quad 4y + 4 &= 4x - 1 &\Rightarrow& &4x - 4y - 5 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

Since normal line is perpendicular to the tangent line, so slope of the **normal line** is $\frac{-1}{1} = -1$.

Equation of normal line is:

$$\begin{aligned} y - (-1) &= -1 \left( x - \frac{1}{4} \right) &\Rightarrow& &y + 1 &= -x + \frac{1}{4} \\ \Rightarrow \quad 4y + 4 &= -4x + 1 &\Rightarrow& &4x + 4y + 3 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

Which is the equation of normal line.

## Exercise 7.5

1. Find the points of intersections of the line $x - 2y + 3 = 0$ and the parabola $y^2 = 8x + 1$. Also find the chord intercepted. Is the chord a focal chord?

2. For what value(s) of $c$ the line $2x + 2y - c = 0$ never touches or intersects at **different** points of the parabola $x^2 - x + 2y + 3 = 0$.

3. Find the value of $a$ so that the line $ax - 2y + 3 = 0$ is tangent to the parabola:
   $$y^2 - 2y + 3x + 7 = 0; \quad a \neq 0$$

4. Prove that the line $3x + y - 5 = 0$ is tangent to the parabola $y^2 - 2y + 6x - 6 = 0$. Also find the point of contact.

5. Find the equation of tangent and normal to the parabola $2y^2 - 3y + 11x - 16 = 0$ at the point $(1, -1)$.

6. Find the equation of tangent and normal to the parabola $x^2 - 5x + 2y + 6 = 0$ at the **point** where abscissa is 1.

7. Find the equation of tangent and normal to the parabola $y^2 = 18x$ at the end points of its latus rectum.

8. Let $\text{P}(at_1^2, 2at_1)$ and $\text{Q}(at_2^2, 2at_2)$ be any two points on the parabola $y^2 = 4ax$. Prove that the chord joining P and Q is a focal chord if $t_1 t_2 = -1$.

9. A tangent line is drawn to the parabola at any point P. Prove that the line segment of the tangent cut off between P and directrix subtends a right angle at the focus.

10. Prove that tangents at the end points of any focal chord intersect at right angles on the directrix.

11. Prove that tangents to the parabola at any point P on the parabola make equal angle with line joining P and its focus and the line through P parallel to the axis of parabola. (Reflecting property of parabola).

12. Prove that the semi latus rectum is a harmonic mean between the segments of any focal chord.
