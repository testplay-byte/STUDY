## 7.10 Equation of Tangent and Normal to Ellipse

### 7.10.1 Condition of Tangency

Consider the ellipse $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$ \hspace{2cm} (1)

and the line \hspace{1cm} $y = mx + c$ \hspace{2cm} (2)

on solving (1) and (2) simultaneously we will get the common points, from (2) put the value of $y$ in equation (1)

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(mx+c-k)^2}{b^2} &= 1 \\
b^2(x-h)^2 + a^2(mx+c-k)^2 &= a^2b^2 \\
b^2(x^2 - 2hx + h^2) + a^2\{m^2x^2 + 2m(c-k) + (c-k)^2\} &= a^2b^2 \\
(b^2 + a^2m^2)x^2 + (-2b^2h + 2a^2m(c-k))x + b^2h^2 + a^2(c-k)^2 - a^2b^2 &= 0
\end{aligned}$$

which is quadratic equation in x.
