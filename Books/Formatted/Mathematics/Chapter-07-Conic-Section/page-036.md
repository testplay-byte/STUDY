**Example 26:** Find the equation of hyperbola with centre at origin with eccentricity $\frac{3}{2}$ and length of its latus rectum is 3.

**Solution:**

Let the equation of hyperbola be

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 \quad \ldots\ldots\ldots(1)$$

Given that $e = \frac{3}{2}$ and $2\frac{b^2}{a} = 3$ $\quad \ldots\ldots\ldots(2)$

Since $c = ae \Rightarrow c = a\left(\frac{3}{2}\right) = \frac{3a}{2}$

also $b^2 = c^2 - a^2 = \left(\frac{3a}{2}\right)^2 - a^2$

$b^2 = \frac{9a^2}{4} - a^2 = \frac{5a^2}{4}$ $\quad \ldots\ldots\ldots(3)$

put in Eq (2)

$\frac{2\left(\frac{5a^2}{4}\right)}{a} = 3 \Rightarrow a = \frac{6}{5} \Rightarrow a^2 = \frac{36}{25}$

put in eq (3)

$b^2 = \frac{5}{4}\left(\frac{36}{25}\right) = \frac{9}{5}$ $\quad \begin{array}{l} \text{put the values of } a^2 \text{ and } b^2 \text{ in Eq (1)} \\[8pt] \Rightarrow \dfrac{x^2}{\left(\dfrac{36}{25}\right)} - \dfrac{y^2}{\left(\dfrac{9}{5}\right)} = 1 \\[15pt] \text{or} \\[5pt] \Rightarrow \dfrac{25x^2}{36} - \dfrac{5y^2}{9} = 1 \\[20pt] \text{is the equation of hyperbola} \end{array}$
