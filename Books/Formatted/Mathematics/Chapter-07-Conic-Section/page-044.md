## 7.16 Use of Conic Section

**Example 34:** The head light of an automobile is in the shape of parabola. The bulb is placed at the focus which is $2 \text{ inch}$ from its vertex. The depth of the head light is $3 \text{ inches}$. What is the width of the head light at its opening?

**Solution:**

Consider the vertex of the parabola is at origin and it opens on the right side. Given that focus is at $2\text{inches}$ from the vertex; so $(a, 0) = (2, 0) \Rightarrow a = 2$

As we know equation of this parabola is $y^{2} = 4ax$ so in this case $$y^{2} = 8x \qquad \ldots (1)$$ Let ‘w’ be the width of the parabola, so coordinates of the points $A$ and $B$ are $A(3, -\frac{w}{2})$ and $B(3, \frac{w}{2})$.

Since the point $B(3, \frac{w}{2})$ (also point $A(3, -\frac{w}{2})$) lies on the parabola, so from Eq (1)

$$\frac{w}{2} = 8(3) \Rightarrow w = 48 \text{ inches}$$

**Example 35:** According to Kepler’s law planets have elliptical orbits with Sun at one of the foci. If the longest distance of the planet from the sun is $4.4 \text{ billion } km$ then write the equation of the orbit of the planet taking its centre at origin.

**Solution:**

Consider the ellipse with centre at origin and its major axis is along $x - axis$. Thus, its equation is $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1 \qquad \ldots (1)$

Let the Sun be at its focus $F_{1}$. The planet will be at maximum distance from Sun when it is at $A_{2}$ and its distance from Sun is minimum when it is at $A_{1}$.
