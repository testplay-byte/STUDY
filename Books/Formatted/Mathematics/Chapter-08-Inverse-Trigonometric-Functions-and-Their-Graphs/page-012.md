## Property II

*   $\text{Cos}(\text{Cos}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in [-1,1]$ and $\text{Sin}(\text{Sin}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in [-1,1]$
*   $\text{Tan}(\text{Tan}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in (-\infty,+\infty)$ and $\text{Cot}(\text{Cot}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in (-\infty,+\infty)$
*   $\text{Sec}(\text{Sec}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$
*   $\text{Csc}(\text{Csc}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$

## Property III

*   $\text{Cos}^{-1}(-x) = \pi - \text{Cos}^{-1}x$ for $x \in [-1,1]$
*   $\text{Sin}^{-1}(-x) = -\text{Sin}^{-1}x$ for $x \in [-1,1]$
*   $\text{Tan}^{-1}(-x) = -\text{Tan}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,+\infty)$
*   $\text{Cot}^{-1}(-x) = \pi - \text{Cot}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,+\infty)$
*   $\text{Sec}^{-1}(-x) = \pi - \text{Sec}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$
*   $\text{Csc}^{-1}(-x) = -\text{Csc}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$

## Property IV

*   $\text{Sin}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \text{Csc}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$
*   $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \text{Sec}^{-1}(x)$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$
*   $\text{Tan}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \begin{cases} \text{Cot}^{-1}(x) & \text{if } x > 0 \\ -\pi + \text{Cot}^{-1}(x) & \text{if } x < 0 \end{cases}$

## Property V

*   $\text{Sin}^{-1}(x) + \text{Cos}^{-1}(x) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ for $x \in [-1,1]$
*   $\text{Tan}^{-1}(x) + \text{Cot}^{-1}(x) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ for $x \in (-\infty,+\infty)$
*   $\text{Sec}^{-1}(-x) + \text{Csc}^{-1}(-x) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ for $x \in (-\infty,-1] \cup [1,\infty)$

## Property VI

*   $\text{Sin}^{-1}(x) = \text{Cos}^{-1}(\sqrt{1-x^2}) = \text{Tan}^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right) = \text{Cot}^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x}\right) = \text{Sec}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right) = \text{Csc}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$
*   $\text{Cos}^{-1}(x) = \text{Sin}^{-1}(\sqrt{1-x^2}) = \text{Tan}^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x}\right) = \text{Cot}^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right) = \text{Sec}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \text{Csc}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)$
*   $\text{Tan}^{-1}(x) = \text{Sin}^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\right) = \text{Cos}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\right) = \text{Cot}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \text{Sec}^{-1}(\sqrt{1+x^2}) = \text{Csc}^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}}{x}\right)$
