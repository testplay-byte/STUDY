*   **Monotonicity:** The graph is monotonically increasing and decreasing smoothly (it always increases/decreases as $x$ increases/increases) along the horizontal asymptote $y = \frac{\pi}{2}$.

*   **Neither even nor odd function**

*   **Reflection:** The graph of $y = \text{Sec}^{-1}x$, is a reflection of $y = \text{Sec } x$ across the line $y = x$. This means that the $x$ and $y$ coordinates of the points on the graph of $y = \text{Sec } x$ are switched to obtain the points on the graph of $y = \text{Sec}^{-1}x$.

*   **Reflection Symmetry:** $\begin{bmatrix} \text{Sec}^{-1}(-x) = \pi - \text{Sec}^{-1}(x) \text{ for } x \in (-\infty, -1] \cup [1, \infty) \\ \quad (\text{symmetric about the line } y = \frac{\pi}{2}) \end{bmatrix}$

    The symmetry along range $[0, \pi] / \{\frac{\pi}{2}\}$ reflects across the vertical axis at $x = 0$.

*   **Inverse Function:** $\text{Sec}(\text{Sec}^{-1}x) = x$ for $x \in (-\infty, -1] \cup [1, \infty)$ and $\text{Sec}^{-1}(\text{Sec } x) = x$ for $x \in [0, \frac{\pi}{2}) \cup (\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi]$

*   **Identities:** $\text{Sec}^{-1}(x) = \text{Cos}^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$ for $x \in (-\infty, -1] \cup [1, \infty)$

## 8.2.6 Graph of $y = \text{Csc}^{-1} x$

The graph of the function $y = \text{Csc}^{-1} x$ represents the angle $y$ whose cosecant is $x$. The domain of this function is $x \in (-\infty, -1] \cup [1, \infty)$, and the range is $y \in [-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0) \cup (0, \frac{\pi}{2}]$. This means the output values $y$ will always be within this interval, corresponding to angles in radians between $0$ and $\pi$.

**Construction Steps:**

*   Draw the coordinate axes. Mark $x$-axis with values from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$ and $y$-axis with values from $0$ to $\pi$ and choose an appropriate scale.
*   Mark the points $(1, \frac{\pi}{2})$, $(1.2, \frac{\pi}{3})$, $(1.4, \frac{\pi}{4})$, $(2, \frac{\pi}{6})$, $(+\infty, +0)$, $(-1, -\frac{\pi}{2})$, $(-1.2, -\frac{\pi}{3})$, $(-1.4, -\frac{\pi}{4})$, $(-2, -\frac{\pi}{6})$, $(-\infty, -0)$ on the graph and connect them smoothly.
