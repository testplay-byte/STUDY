## 8.3 Addition and Subtraction Formulas for Inverse Trigonometric Functions

For any real numbers $x$ and $y$ within the domain of the inverse trigonometric functions, the addition and subtraction formulas can be derived from the addition/subtraction formulas of their corresponding trigonometric functions. Here are the key formulas:

### 8.3.1 Inverse Sine Addition and Subtraction Formulas

(i) $\text{Sin}^{-1}(x) + \text{Sin}^{-1}(y) = \text{Sin}^{-1}[x\sqrt{1 - y^2} + y\sqrt{1 - x^2}]$,

(ii) $\text{Sin}^{-1}(x) - \text{Sin}^{-1}(y) = \text{Sin}^{-1}[x\sqrt{1 - y^2} - y\sqrt{1 - x^2}]$

where $x,y \in [-1,1]$ and $xy \in (-\infty, 1]$

**Proof:**

(i) Let $\alpha = \text{Sin}^{-1}(x)$ if and only if $x = \text{Sin } \alpha$, where $\alpha \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ and $x \in [-1,1]$

Let $\beta = \text{Sin}^{-1}(y)$ if and only if $y = \text{Sin } \beta$, where $\beta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ and $y \in [-1,1]$
