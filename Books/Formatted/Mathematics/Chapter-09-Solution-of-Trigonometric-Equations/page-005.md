## 9.2 Graphical Solution of the Trigonometric Equations

Graphical solutions are particularly useful, especially for visualizing and understanding the behavior of trigonometric functions. By plotting trigonometric functions on graph, we can observe that how changes in parameters affect the amplitude (height) and phase shift (horizontal translation) of the trigonometric functions, providing insight into how these functions model real-world phenomena like sound waves or tides.
