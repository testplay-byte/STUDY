**Example 6:**

Solve graphically the trigonometric equation: $\cos\theta = \theta$ where $\theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$.

**Solution:**

In order to solve a trigonometric equation graphically, we follow as:

Graph the function $\cos\theta = \theta$ where $\theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

Step-I: Let $y = \cos \theta$ and $y = \theta$

Step-II: Table of values for $y = \cos\theta$

| $\theta$ | $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $y = \cos \theta$ | $0$ | $0.50$ | $0.71$ | $0.87$ | $1$ | $0.87$ | $0.71$ | $0.50$ | $0$ |

Step-III: Table of values for $y = \theta$

| $\theta$ | $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $y = \theta$ | $-1.57$ | $-1.05$ | $-0.79$ | $-0.52$ | $0$ | $0.52$ | $0.79$ | $1.05$ | $1.57$ |

Step IV: Scale along $x$-axis: 1 small square = $3^\circ$
Scale along $y$-axis: 1 small square = 0.1 units

[Figure F2]

**Analyzing the Graph**

The sine wave $(y = \cos \theta)$ oscillates and remains bounded between $-1$ and $1$.

The straight line $(y = \theta)$ has slope 1.

It increases linearly and crosses $x$-axis at $x = 0$.

**Finding the Intersection Point**

Graph of $y = \cos\theta$ intersects the graph of $y = \theta$ at $x = 0$.

Solution set $= \left\{ \frac{53\pi}{225} rad \text{ or } 0.74 rad \text{ or } 42.4^\circ \right\}$
