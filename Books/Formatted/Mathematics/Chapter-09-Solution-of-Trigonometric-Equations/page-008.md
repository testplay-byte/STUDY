**Example 7:**

Solve graphically the trigonometric equation: $\tan\theta = 2\theta$ where $\theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$.

**Solution:**

In order to solve a trigonometric equation graphically, we follow as:

Graph the function $\tan\theta = 2\theta$ where $\theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

Step-I: Let $y = \tan \theta$ and $y = 2\theta$

Step-II: Table of values for $y = \tan \theta$

| $\theta$ | $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $y = \tan\theta$ | $-\infty$ | $-1.73$ | $-1$ | $-0.58$ | $0$ | $0.58$ | $1$ | $1.73$ | $+\infty$ |

Step-III: Table of values for $y = 2\theta$

| $\theta$ | $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $2\theta$ | $-\pi$ | $-\frac{2\pi}{3}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $-\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $\frac{2\pi}{3}$ | $\pi$ |
| $y = 2\theta$ | $-3.14$ | $-2.09$ | $-1.57$ | $-1.05$ | $0$ | $1.05$ | $1.57$ | $2.09$ | $+3.14$ |

Step IV: Scale along $x$-axis: 1 small square $= \frac{\pi}{60}$ rad or $3^\circ$
Scale along $y$-axis: 1 small square $= 0.1$ units

**Analyzing the Graph**

The tangent curve ($y = \tan \theta$) is periodic with vertical asymptotes $x = -\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $x = \frac{\pi}{2}$. It starts from $-\infty$ just before $-\frac{\pi}{2}$, crosses the origin, and then approaches $\infty$ just before $\frac{\pi}{2}$. The straight line ($y = 2\theta$) increases linearly with slope 2 and crosses $x$-axis through origin

**Finding the Intersection Point**

Graph of $y = 2\theta$ intersects the graph of $y = \tan\theta$ at $x = 0, \pm66.82^\circ$.
Solution set $= \{0, \pm1.17 \text{ rad or } \pm 66.82^\circ\}$
