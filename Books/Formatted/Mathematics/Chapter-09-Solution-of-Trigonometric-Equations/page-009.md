   i. $\cos\theta = \frac{\theta}{2} ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   ii. $\tan\theta = \frac{\theta}{2} ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   iii. $\sin\theta = \theta ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   iv. $\tan\theta = \theta ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   v. $\sin\theta = 2\theta ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   vi. $\cos\theta = 2\theta ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

   vii. $\sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) = \theta ; \theta \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

## 9.3 Daily Life Applications of Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Inverse trigonometric functions have various practical applications in daily life, especially in fields that involve measurement, navigation, and engineering. Here are some examples:

**Example 8:** A mechanical arm in a factory is required to reach an object 5 meters away and 2 meters above its base. Find the angle at which the arm should be positioned using the suitable inverse function.

**Solution:** In the figure, object is placed at point C and mechanical arm is positioned at point A.

In right triangle ABC:

$$\begin{aligned} \tan\theta &= \frac{BC}{AB} = \frac{2}{5} \\ \Rightarrow \quad \theta &= \text{Tan}^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{5}\right) = \text{Tan}^{-1}(0.4) = 21.8^{\circ} \end{aligned}$$

Therefore, angle that the mechanical arm makes with the base is $21.8^{\circ}$.

An engineer needs to design a bridge that makes a $7^{\circ}$ angle with the horizontal. If the bridge spans 300 meters across a river, find the height difference between the two ends of the bridge using an appropriate inverse function.
