# Page 10 — Unit 09: Solution of Trigonometric Equations

> 📄 Original scan: [0010.jpg](../../../Raw/Mathematics/Unit-09-Solution-of-Trigonometric-Equations/0010.jpg) · printed page 271

Let $\text{QR} = x$ m

In right triangle PQR:

$$\frac{\text{QR}}{\text{PQ}} = \sin \theta \implies \frac{x}{300} = \sin 7^\circ$$

$$\implies x = 300 \times \sin 7^\circ = 36.56 m$$

Therefore, height difference between two ends of the bridge is $36.56$ m.

## Exercise 9.3

1. A surveyor is standing 50 meters away from a building and measures the angle of elevation to the top of the building as $35^\circ$. What is the height of the building? Use the inverse tangent function to find the height.

2. A pedestrian bridge is to be constructed with a slope of $5^\circ$ to meet accessibility requirements. If the horizontal distance between the two ends of the bridge is 120 meters, determine the vertical height difference using the inverse sine function.

3. A ramp is to be constructed to a platform 2 meters high. If the ramp is 5 meters long, calculate the angle of elevation of the ramp.

4. A conveyor belt in a factory is inclined at an angle of $18^\circ$. If the conveyor belt needs to move goods to a height of 6 meters, find the horizontal distance covered by the conveyor belt using the inverse tangent function.

5. A lighthouse is 120 meters tall, and a ship is spotted at a distance of 250 meters from the base of the lighthouse. Find the angle of depression from the lighthouse to the ship using the inverse tangent function.

6. A lighthouse is 90 meters tall, and a boat is observed at a distance of 180 meters from the base of the lighthouse. Determine the angle of elevation from the boat to the top of the lighthouse using the inverse tangent function.

7. For optimal energy absorption, a solar panel needs to be installed at an angle where it directly faces the sun at noon, which is at a $48^\circ$ angle from the horizontal. If the panel is placed 2 meters above the ground, calculate the length of the supporting rod required using an inverse sine function.

8. In constructing a roof, the horizontal span is 10 meters, and the desired height of the roof's peak is 3 meters. Determine the angle at which the roof trusses should be installed using an inverse cosine function.

9. A cable supporting a 15meter high tower forms a $60^\circ$ angle with the ground. Determine the length of the cable needed to support the tower using the inverse cosine function.
