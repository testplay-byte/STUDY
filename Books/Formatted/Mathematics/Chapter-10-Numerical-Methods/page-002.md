## 10.1 Importance of Numerical Methods

Numerical methods are indeed crucial for scientists and engineers because they provide tools to solve mathematical problems that cannot be tackled through direct or analytical methods. In many cases, the exact solutions for certain mathematical models, especially those involving complex integrals, differential equations, or large systems of linear equations, are not available or are too difficult to obtain. Numerical methods offer approximations that are often sufficiently accurate for practical purposes.

For example, consider an integral that is challenging to solve analytically:

$$ \int e^{-x^2} dx $$

This integral, known as the Gaussian integral, does not have an elementary antiderivative, making it impossible to express its solution in terms of basic functions. However, numerical methods such as the Trapezoidal Rule, Simpson's Rule, or Gaussian Quadrature can be used to approximate its value with a high degree of accuracy.

In a similar way while emphasizing the importance of numerical methods, especially when it comes to solving non-linear equations or other complex mathematical problems that are either impossible or impractical to solve analytically. In many scientific and engineering applications, the equations involved are too complicated to yield exact solutions. For instance, non-linear equations like:

$$ \sin(x) + e^x + \ln(x) + x = 0 $$

are often not solvable using simple algebraic methods. Instead, numerical techniques like the Newton-Raphson method, bisection method, or secant method are employed to find approximate solutions. These methods iteratively refine the solution, bringing it closer to the exact value with each step.

In applied sciences, numerical methods allow engineers and scientists to model and simulate real-world phenomena, optimize designs, and solve equations that describe physical, chemical, and biological systems. Without these methods, many practical problems would remain unsolved or would require overly simplified models that might not accurately reflect the complexities of the real world.

Thus, numerical methods are indispensable for advancing technology, conducting research, and solving complex engineering problems.

## 10.2 The Role of Numerical Methods

Numerical methods are crucial because they:

i. **Approximate Solutions:** When exact solutions are unattainable, numerical methods provide close approximations, which are often sufficient for practical purposes.

ii. **Automate Repetitive Computations:** These methods transform complex problems into a series of simpler, repetitive calculations. This is particularly suited for computers, which can perform these operations quickly and without errors.
