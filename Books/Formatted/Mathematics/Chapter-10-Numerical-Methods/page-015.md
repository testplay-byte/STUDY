## Exercise 10.2

1. The values of a certain function $f(x)$ are given in the following table.

| x | 0 | 0.5 | 1 | 1.5 | 2 | 2.5 | 3 | 3.5 | 4 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| f(x) | 1 | 1.649 | 2.718 | 4.482 | 7.389 | 12.18 | 20.09 | 22.12 | 45.60 |

By using Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rule to compute the integral $\int\limits_{0}^{4} f(x)dx$ .

2. Evaluate $\int\limits_{-1}^{7} f(x)dx$ using Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rules.

| x | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| f(x) | 0.9848 | 1 | 0.9848 | 0.9397 | 0.8660 | 0.7660 | 0.6428 | 0.500 | 0.3420 |

3. Compute $\int\limits_{0}^{1} \frac{dx}{2+x^2}$ by Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rules with $n = 8$. Also evaluate the function analytically and comments on the outcomes in each case.

4. Evaluate $\int\limits_{0}^{1} \frac{dx}{1+x}$ using Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rules for $n = 8$. Compare your outcomes with exact value.

5. Evaluate $\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin(x)dx$ using the Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rules by taking $n=6$.

6. Evaluate the integral $\int\limits_{0}^{3} \frac{x^2}{1+x} dx$ using Trapezoidal and Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rules by taking $n = 10$.
