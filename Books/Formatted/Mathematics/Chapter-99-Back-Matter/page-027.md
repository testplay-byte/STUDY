## ABOUT AUTHORS

*Dr. Khalid Mahmood*

He has a long teaching experience of more than two decades at school and college level. He holds the degree of Ph.D (Maths) along with professional degree of M.Ed. Presently, he is working as Principal and Area Education Officer(AEO) in ICT. He has authored and reviewed many books in the subject of mathematics at different level. He has worked as a resource person for imparting training to teachers at elementary and secondary levels.
Email: khalidmeh2012@gmail.com

*Dr. Saleem Ullah Satti*

Dr. Saleem Ullah Satti possesses Ph.D (Maths) along with different professional teaching degrees. Presently, he is working in mathematics department Air University, Islamabad. He has experience of two decades in teaching of mathematics at various levels. He authored and reviewed many books. He has been involved in the development of curriculum for mathematics upto higher level. As a resource person, he has conducted training programmes, workshops and seminars on the subject.
Email: saleemullah314@hotmail.com

*Muhammad Dabeer Mughal*

Muhammad Dabeer Mughal is a seasoned and able mathematician who has been teaching mathematics at school, college, and BS level since 2001. Currently, he is pursuing his Ph.D. in Mathematics. He did his M. Phil. (Mathematics) in 2019. He is an excellent brain and has earned great repute among notable mathematicians in the country. He has been an active member of the National Curriculum Council (NCC) and has reviewed many books since 2019. He has always been taken as a great asset for his valuable contributions.

*Dr. Naveed Akmal*

He has an experience of 2 decades in teaching mathematics at the College and graduate level. He has done his M. Phil. (Mathematics) from Ripah University with the distinction of achieving a Gold Medal. He is a Ph.D. in Mathematics specialization in Fluid Mechanics. He has been working as a reviewer and evaluator of school and college textbooks for the last 10 years. He has been an active member of the National Curriculum Council (NCC), designed and developed the National Curriculum-(VI-VIII) in 2018.

*Muhammad Anwar ul Haq*

Assistant Professor, Assistant Vice Principal, and Head of the Maths Department at Bahria College E-8 Islamabad, boasts over 30+ years of teaching excellence at HSSC and BS levels. Holding an MSc (Applied Mathematics), PGD in Computer Science, and B.Ed, he contributed to Pakistan’s National Curricula (2006, 2022-23), reviewed 100+ books for the National Curriculum Council, served as a Master Trainer since 2004, training hundreds in assessment methodologies and designed model papers for FBISE. Honored with the 2023 Best Teacher Award.
