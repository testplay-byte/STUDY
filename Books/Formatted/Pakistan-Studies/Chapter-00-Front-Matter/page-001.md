## Figures on this page

### Figure F1 — Front cover art (upper two-thirds of cover)
- **Type:** photo (composite cover art)
- **Caption/Number:** (none printed)
- **Description:** Cover art on a pale, overcast grey-white sky with soft cumulus clouds. Centred is a photographic rendering in greyscale of the **Minar-e-Pakistan** (Lahore): a tall fluted/ribbed concrete minaret tapering upward to a domed cupola with a thin finial, rising from a petal-shaped base of large curved flower-petal-like arches with arched openings. Superimposed behind the tower is a large solid **dark-green crescent moon** (wrapping from about 9 o'clock to 3–4 o'clock around the shaft) with **three small white bird silhouettes in flight** inside its left curve, and a solid dark-green **five-pointed star** at the right of the tower, partially overlapping it. A flock of roughly 15–20 small black bird silhouettes flies upward-right between the tower and the "12" box. Foreground: at lower left a Pakistani national flag (green field, white hoist stripe, white crescent and star) on a thin pole, in front of dark-green foliage that partially obscures the tower base; a single dense dark-green deciduous tree stands at the lower right; a faint cityscape silhouette shows on the far-left horizon. Everything sits on a light grey ground plane with subtle shadows.
- **Meaning:** Cover art symbolising national identity through the iconic Minar-e-Pakistan and the flag, crescent and star of Pakistan.

### Figure F2 — Bottom publisher banner (dark-green band across the full cover width)
- **Type:** illustration (logo/emblem banner)
- **Caption/Number:** (none printed)
- **Description:** A solid dark forest-green horizontal band across the bottom of the cover. **Left:** the circular National Book Foundation logo — a red/orange gradient disc with a white stylised bird in flight carrying a black book, ringed by a blue circular border bearing the curved legends "BOOKS GIVE US WINGS" (top arc) and "NATIONAL BOOK FOUNDATION" (bottom arc). **Centre:** the publisher line in white/cream serif type on three lines — "National Book Foundation" / "as" (smaller) / "Federal Textbook Board, Islamabad" — transcribed in the body above. **Right:** the State Emblem of Pakistan in white/cream line art: crescent and star at the apex, a quartered shield (cotton, wheat, tea and jute motifs), a wreath of jasmine surrounding the shield, and a scroll at the base with a motto that is not legible at scan resolution.
- **Meaning:** Publisher and Federal Textbook Board identification marks.
