## Glossary

*   **Award:** A decision of arbitrator.
*   **Field Marshal:** A senior military rank equivalent to five star general.
*   **Governor General:** The highest British official in India.
*   **Khilafat:** Islamic system of government led by a caliph.
*   Princely States: Semi-autonomous regions under British Raj in India ruled by local Rajas.
*   **Simla:** The summer capital of British India.
*   **British Raj:** The period of British rule over India from 1858 to 1947.
*   **Ottoman Empire:** Muslim empire centered in Turkey, lasting from the 13th to early 20th century.
