# Political Development in Pakistan

**In this unit the students will be able to:**

*   Evaluate the contribution and leadership of Quaid-e-Azam as the first Governor General and Liaquat Ali Khan as the first Prime Minister of Pakistan.
*   Critically analyse the ruling periods of different governments, evaluating their policies and their impact on Pakistan's political, social and economic development from 1958 till 2022.
*   Critically analyse the causes and impacts of the Wars of 1948, 1965, 1971 and 199 between Pakistan and India.
*   Analyse the social, political and economic impacts of international wars (War on Terror and Soviet-Afghan war) on Pakistan.
