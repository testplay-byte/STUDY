## Mineral Resources

A mineral resource refers to a concentrated deposit of naturally occurring solid, liquid, or gaseous material that is located on or within the Earth's surface. This material is present in a sufficient amount and form that it may be cheaply extracted and converted into valuable materials.

Minerals are essential for various biological and industrial processes. For instance, calcium and phosphorus are crucial for bone formation in living organisms, while minerals like quartz, feldspar, and mica are vital components in manufacturing materials such as glass, ceramics, and electronics. Similarly minerals like iron and coal are important in different ways.

## Importance of Minerals

> **Expand Your Horizon**
>
> Minerals are essential for modern life, powering everything from smartphones to skyscrapers. Understanding minerals helps us appreciate their crucial role in our daily lives and inspires sustainable innovation for the future. Explore how minerals shape our world!

Mineral resources are of immense importance to modern society due to their widespread use in various industries and everyday life. They serve as essential raw materials for manufacturing, construction, energy production, and technological advancements.

For example, minerals like iron, copper, and aluminum are crucial for producing vehicles, machinery, and infrastructure. Precious metals such as gold and silver are not only used in jewelry but also have significant applications in electronics and finance. Energy minerals like coal, uranium, and lithium are vital for electricity generation and powering batteries.

Furthermore, minerals contribute to economic development by creating jobs, driving innovation, and supporting trade. Their sustainable management and extraction are critical to ensuring a continuous supply for future generations, highlighting the need for responsible mining practices and environmental stewardship.

## Mineral Resources in Pakistan

Pakistan possesses a wide range of mineral resources that are distributed throughout different parts of the country. The following are the basic types of minerals found in Pakistan:  
i) Metallic Minerals  
ii) Non-metallic Minerals  
iii) Energy Minerals  
iv) Gemstones

## Metallic Minerals

Minerals that have a metallic appearance or properties are called metallic minerals. Significant reserves of iron ore are located in Chiniot and Kalabagh in Punjab, as well as in the Nokundi region in Balochistan. Iron ore is mainly used for steel production.
