**Q.8** A random variable X has the probability distribution:

| x | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| p(x) | a | 3a | 5a | 7a | 9a | 11a | 13a | 15a | 17a |

(i) Determine the value of a. (ii) Find $P(X < 2)$, $P(X \geq 6)$, $P(3 < X < 5)$.

**Ans.** (i) 1/81 (ii) 4/81, 5/9, 1/9

**Q.9** Find (i) $E(X)$ (ii) $E(X^2)$ (iii) $E[X - E(X)]^2$ for the following probability distribution.

| x | – 10 | – 20 | 30 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| p( x ) | 1/5 | 3/10 | 1/2 |

**Ans.** (i) 7 (ii) 590 (iii) 541

**Q.10** Let X have the following probability distribution:

| x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| p(x) | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.1 |

Find $E(3X - 1)$, $E(X^2)$ and $E(X^2 + 2)$.

**Ans.** 7.1, 8.9, 10.9

**Q.11** Let X be a random variable with probability distribution as follows:

| x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| f(x) | 0.125 | 0.450 | 0.250 | 0.050 | 0.125 |

Show that $E(5X + 8) = 5E(X) + 8$.

**Ans.** 21

**Q.12** A random variable X has the following probability distribution:

| Value of X | Probability Distribution of X, P(X = x) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | P(X = 1) = 6/9 |
| 2 | P(X = 2) = 2/9 |
| 3 | P(X = 3) = 1/9 |

(i) Determine the expected value of X (ii) Determine the variance of X (iii) Determine the standard deviation of X.

**Ans.** (i) 1.444 (ii) 0.471 (iii) 0.686

**Q.13** Given the following discrete probability distribution:

| x | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| p(x) | 6/36 | 10/36 | 8/36 | 6/36 | 4/36 | 2/36 |

Compute its mean, variance, standard deviation and coefficient of variation.

**Ans.** 1.944, 2.054, 1.433, 73.71 %

**Q.14** If $f(x) = \frac{6 - |7 - x|}{36}$ for $x = 2, 3, 4, \ldots, 12$, then find the mean and variance of the random variable X.

**Ans.** 7, 5.83

**Q.15** A random variable X takes the values – 3, – 2, 2, 3 and 4 with probabilities p(x) equal to 1/5, 1/10, 1/10, 1/5 and 2/5 respectively. Compute $E(X)$, $\text{Var}(X)$ and $\text{Var}(5X + 10)$

**Ans.** $E( X ) = 1.6$, $\text{Var}( X ) = 8.24$ and $\text{Var}( 5X + 10 ) = 206$
