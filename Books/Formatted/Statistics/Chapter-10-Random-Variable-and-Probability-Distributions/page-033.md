**Q.16** Let X be a random variable with probability distribution:

| x | -1 | 0. | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| p( x ) | 0.125 | 0.500 | 0.200 | 0.050 | 0.125 |

Find the probability distribution of the random variable $Y = 2X + 1$. Using the probability distribution of Y, determine $E(Y)$ and $\text{Var}(Y)$.

**Ans.**

| y | -1 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | E(Y) = 2.1 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| p(y) | 0.125 | 0.500 | 0.200 | 0.050 | 0.125 | Var(Y) = 5.39 |

**Q.17** Given a random variable X with $E(X) = 5$ and another random variable Y with $E(Y) = 10$. Find $E(X + 2Y)$ and $E(12 - 2X)$.

**Ans.** 25, 2

**Q.18** If $E(X) = 5$, $E(X^2) = 36$, find the mean and variance of $2X - 5$.

**Ans.** 5, 44

**Q.19** If a pair of fair dice is rolled and X denotes the sum of the numbers on them, then find the expectation of X. If only a single die is rolled what is the expectation of a number on the die.

**Ans.** 7, 3.5

**Q.20** A person plays a game of tossing a die under the condition that he would get twice as many rupees as the number of points on the upper face. Find his expectation.

**Ans.** 7

**Q.22** Approximately 10 % of the glass bottles coming from a production line have serious defects. If two bottles are selected at random, find the expected number of bottles that have serious defects.

**Ans.** $E( X ) = 0.2$

**Q.23** What is the expectation of a person who is to get Rs.80 if he obtains 3 heads in a single toss of 3 coins.

**Ans.** Rs.10

**Q.24** A committee of size 5 is to be selected at random from 3 women and 5 men. Find the expected number of women on the committee.

**Ans.** 2 approximately

**Q.25** Three balls are drawn from a bag containing 5 white and 3 black balls. If X denotes the number of white balls drawn from the bag, then find the probability distribution of X. Also find its mean and variance.

**Q.26** A man draws 2 balls from a bag containing 3 white and 5 black balls. If he is to receive Rs.70 for every white ball which he draws and Rs.7 for every black ball, find his expectation.

**Ans.** Rs.61.25
