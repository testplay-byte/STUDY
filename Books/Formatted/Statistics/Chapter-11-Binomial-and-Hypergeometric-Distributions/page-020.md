# Page 20 — Binomial and Hypergeometric Distributions (Chapter 11)

> 📄 Original scan: [0020.jpg](../../../Raw/Statistics/Chapter-11-Binomial-and-Hypergeometric-Distributions/0020.jpg) · printed page 114

**21.** Which of the following is not property of a binomial experiment?
(a) Probability of success remains constant (b) n is fixed
(c) Successive trials are dependent (d) It has two parameters

**22.** The binomial probability distribution is symmetrical when:
(a) $p = q = 1/2$ (b) $p < q$ (c) $p > q$ (d) $np > npq$

**23.** The binomial distribution is negatively skewed if:
(a) $p < 1/2$ (b) $p = 1/2$ (c) $p > 1/2$ (d) $p = 1$

**24.** In a binomial probability distribution, the skewness is positive for:
(a) $p < 1/2$ (b) $p = 1/4$ (c) $np = npq$ (d) $np = nq$

**25.** Which one of the following statements is false?
(a) Expected value of a constant is the constant.
(b) In a binomial distribution the standard deviation is always less than its variance.
(c) In a binomial probability distribution the mean is always greater than its variance.
(d) In binomial experiment the probability of success remains constant from trial to trial.

**26.** If a binomial probability distribution has parameters $(n, p) = (5, 0.6)$, the probability of $X = 3.5$ is:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 0.6 (d) 0.4

**27.** In a binomial experiment $n = 4$, $P(X = 2) = 216/625$ and $P(X = 3) = 216/625$ then $P( X = -2 )$ is equal to:
(a) 216/625 (b) 1 (c) 0 (d) difficult to tell

**28.** If $n = 6$ and $p = 0.9$, then the value of $P( X = 7 )$ is:
(a) zero (b) less than zero (c) more than zero (d) one

**29.** In binomial probability distribution if $n = 3$, then $P( X > 3 )$ is equal to:
(a) zero (b) one (c) minus one (d) less than zero

**30.** For a binomial distribution with $n = 5$, $P( X = -5 )$ is equal to:
(a) 0 (b) < 0 (c) > 0 (d) 1

**31.** For a binomial distribution with $n = 10$, $p = 0.5$, the probability of zero or more successes is:
(a) 1 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.25 (d) 0.75

**32.** In a binomial distribution, the mean, median and mode coincide when:
(a) $p < 1/2$ (b) $p > 1/2$ (c) $p \neq 1/2$ (d) $p = 1/2$

**33.** In which distribution, the probability of success remains constant from trial to trial?
(a) Hypergeometric distribution (b) Binomial distribution
(c) Sampling distribution (d) Frequency distribution

**34.** In a binomial experiment when $n = 5$, the maximum number of successes will be:
(a) 0 (b) 2.5 (c) 4 (d) 5

**35.** In a binomial experiment when $n = 10$, the minimum number of successes will be:
(a) 0 (b) 5 (c) 10 (d) 11

**36.** If $n = 10$ and $p = 0.6$, then $P( X \geq 0 )$ is:
(a) 0.5 (b) 0.6 (c) 1.0 (d) 1.2

**37.** A random variable X has a binomial distribution with $n = 4$, the standard deviation of X is:
(a) $4pq$ (b) $2\sqrt{pq}$ (c) $4p$ (d) $4(q + p)$

**38.** In a multiple choice test there are five possible answers to each of 20 questions. If a candidate guesses the correct answer each time, the mean number of correct answers is:
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 1/5 (d) 20

**39.** If three coins are tossed, the probability of two heads is:
(a) 1/8 (b) 3/8 (c) 2/3 (d) 0

**40.** A random variable X has binomial distribution with $n = 8$ and $p = 1/2$. The most probable value of X is:
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

**41.** The value of second moment about the mean in a binomial distribution is 36. The value of the standard deviation of a binomial distribution is:
(a) 36 (b) 6 (c) 1/36 (d) 1/6
