**42.** For a binomial probability distribution, the expected frequency of x successes in N experiments is:

(a) symmetrical (b) skewed to the left (c) skewed to the right (d) asymmetrical

**48.** The variance of binomial probability distribution $(q + p)^3$ is equal to:
(a) $3pq$ (b) $\sqrt{3pq}$ (c) $3p$ (d) $3p + q$

**54.** The range of hypergeometric distribution is:
(a) $0$ to **n** (b) $0$ to **k** (c) $0$ to **N** (d) $0$ to **n** or **k** (whichever is less)

**55.** The number of trials in hypergeometric distribution is:
(a) not fixed (b) fixed (c) large (d) small

**56.** The probability of a success changes from trial to trial in:
(a) binomial distribution (b) hypergeometric distribution

**57.** The mean of the hypergeometric distribution is:
(a) $\frac{nk}{N}$ (b) $\frac{Nk}{n}$  
(c) $\frac{Nn}{k}$ (d) $\frac{n+k}{N}$

**58.** The standard deviation of the hypergeometric distribution is:
(a) $\frac{nk(k-N)(N-n)}{N^2(N-1)}$ (b) $\sqrt{\frac{nk(N-k)(N-n)}{N^2(N-1)}}$

(c) $\sqrt{\frac{nk(N-k)(n-N)}{N^2(N+1)}}$ (d) $\sqrt{\frac{nk(N-k)+(N-n)}{N^2(N-1)}}$

**59.** The variance of hypergeometric distribution is equal to:
(a) $\left(\frac{nk}{N}\right)\left(\frac{N-k}{N}\right)\left(\frac{N-n}{N-1}\right)$ (b) $\left(\frac{nk}{N}\right)\left(\frac{k-N}{N}\right)\left(\frac{N-n}{N-1}\right)$
