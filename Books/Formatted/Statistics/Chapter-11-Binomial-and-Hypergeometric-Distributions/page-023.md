**Q.21** 5 % of items in a large batch are defective. If 50 items are selected at random, find the probability that at least one will be defective.

**Ans.** 0.9231

**Q.22** A supermarket stocks eggs are in boxes of six, and 10 % of the eggs are cracked. Assuming that the cracked eggs are distributed at random, compute the probability that a customer will find that the first box he chooses contains no cracked egg.

**Ans.** 0.5314

**Q.23** It is known that 40 % of the defective parts produced in a certain manufacturing process can be made satisfactory by rework. Find the probability that in a batch of six such defective parts none of them can be reworked.

**Ans.** 0.0467

**Q.24** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 3$ and $p = 1/2$. Find $P(X > 3)$ and $P(X = -1)$.

**Ans.** 0 and 0

**Q.25** In a binomial experiment $n = 5$, $P(X = 1) = 5/32$ and $P(X = 2) = 10/32$. Find $P(X = 5)$.

**Ans.** 1/32

**Q.26** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 4$ and $P(X = 2) = 3P(X = 3)$. Find $p$

**Ans.** 1/3

**Q.27** A random variable $X$ is believed to follow a binomial distribution with $b(x, 5, p)$. If $P(X = 0) = 243/1024$, find $P(X = 3)$.

**Ans.** 0.0879

**Q.28** If $n = 10$ and $p = 0.4$, then find mean, variance and coefficient of variation of binomial distribution.

**Ans.** 4, 2.4, and 38.75%

**Q.29** If the probability that a day is fine is 0.6. Find the expected number and standard deviation of the fine days in a week.

**Ans.** (4.2, 1.30)

**Q.30** In a binomial distribution with $n = 5$, $P(X = 0) = P(X = 1)$. Find the variance.

**Ans.** 25/36

**Q.31** It is estimated that 1 in 20 students are left-handed. What sample size should be taken to ensure that the expected number of left-handed students in the sample is 3? Also find the value of parameter $\sigma$.

**Ans.** $n = 60$ and $\sigma$

**Q.32** In a multiple choice test there are five possible answers to each of 20 questions. If a candidate guesses the correct answer each time, find standard deviation of correct answers.

**Ans.** 1.79

**Q.33** If $X \sim b(20, 2/5)$. Find mean and variance of binomial distribution.

**Ans.** 8 and 4.8

**Q.34** Find mean and variance of the given binomial distribution $\left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3}\right)^{12}$.

**Ans.** 8 and 2.6667

**Q.35** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 20$ and $p = 1/2$. Find $P(X = \mu - \sigma^2)$.

**Ans.** 0.2461

**Q.36** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 25$ and $p = 0.2$. Find $P(X < \mu - 2\sigma)$.

**Ans.** 0.0038

**Q.37** If a binomial probability distribution has parameters $(n, p) = (20, 0.6)$. Find the expected number of successes and standard deviation.

**Ans.** (12; 2.19)

**Q.38** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 10$ and $p = 0.4$. Find $S.D.(3 - 2X)$.

**Ans.** 3.1

**Q.39** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with $n = 10$ and $p = 0.4$. Find mean and variance of $Y = \dfrac{3 - 2X}{5}$.

**Ans.** -1 and 0.384

**Q.40** If $X$ is binomially distributed with $n = 10$ and $p = 0.4$.Find the variance of $Y = (X - 10)/6$.

**Ans.** 0.0667

**Q.41** If $\mu = np = 2$ and $q = 0.90$. Find $n$.

**Ans.** $n = 20$.

**Q.42** In a binomial probability distribution mean = 4 and $q = 0.84$. Find $n$ and $p$.

**Ans.** $n = 25$ and $p = 0.16$.

**Q.43** Discuss the statement that in a binomial distribution mean = 5 and standard deviation = 5.

**Ans.** Impossible because $q = 5$.

**Q.44** Discuss the statement that in a binomial distribution mean = 5 and variance = 5.

**Ans.** Since $q$ is a probability of failure which is equal to one. In this case $p = 0$ and $n$ is undefined, therefore the given statement is incorrect.
