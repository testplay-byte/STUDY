**Q.45** Is it possible to have binomial distribution with mean 10 and standard deviation 3?

**Ans.** Yes, mean > variance

**Q.46** The mean and second moment about the mean in a binomial distribution are 10 and 6 respectively. Find the value of $n$.

**Ans.** 25

**Q.47** If $X$ is a binomial random variable with mean = 1.44 and standard deviation = 0.96. Find $P(X = 2)$.

**Ans.** 0.3185

**Q.48** A random variable $X$ has binomial distribution with $E(X) = 2.4$ and $p = 0.3$. Find the standard deviation of $X$.

**Ans.** 1.30

**Q.50** In a binomial distribution consisting of 5 independent trials, probabilities of 1 and 2 successes are 0.4096 and 0.2048 respectively. Find the expected value.
