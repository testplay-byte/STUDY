**Q.39** What is the probability of drawing five cards from a deck and getting:
(i) three spades?　　(ii) at most two spades?　　(iii) at least three spades?

**Ans.** (i) 0.0815　　(ii) 0.9072　　(iii) 0.0927

**Q.40** From a lot of 10 missiles, 4 are selected at random and fired. If the lot contains 3 defective missiles that will not fire, what is the probability that

(i) all 4 will fire?　　(ii) at least 3 will fire?　　(iii) at most 2 will not fire?　　(iv) at least 2 will not fire?

**Ans.** (i) 0.1667　　(ii).6667　　(iii) 0.9667　　(iv) .3333
