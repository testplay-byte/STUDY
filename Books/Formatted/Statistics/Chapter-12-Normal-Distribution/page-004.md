**Example 12.2.**
In a normal distribution, mean is zero and the standard deviation is one. Write down its equation and find the value of the maximum ordinate correct to four places of decimal.

**Solution:** The equation of the normal curve with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$ is

$$Y = f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2} \text{ for } -\infty < X < \infty$$

When $\mu = 0$ and $\sigma = 1$, the equation of the normal curve will be

$$Y = f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}$$

We know that the maximum ordinate is at $X = \mu$ and $\mu = 0$, the value of the maximum ordinate is

$$Y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}(0)^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2(3.1416)}} = \frac{1}{2.5066} = 0.3989 \quad (\text{since } e^0 = 1)$$

**Example 12.3.**
Find the area under the normal curve in each of the cases:
(i) Between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 1.2$  
(ii) Between $Z = -0.68$ and $Z = 0$  
(iii) Between $Z = -0.46$ and $Z = 2.21$  
(iv) Between $Z = 0.81$ and $Z = 1.94$  
(v) To the left of $Z = -0.6$  
(vi) To the right of $Z = -1.28$  
(vii) To the right of $Z = 2.05$ and to the left of $Z = - 1.44$.

**Solution:**

(i) Between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 1.2$

Required area = Area between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 1.2$ is 0.3849 [Figure F1]

(ii) Between $Z = -0.68$ and $Z = 0$

Required area = Area between $Z = -0.68$ and $Z = 0$ is 0.2518 [Figure F2]

(iii) Between $Z = -0.46$ and $Z = 2.21$

Required area = (Area between $Z = -0.46$ and $Z = 0$)  
+ (Area between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 2.21$)  
= $0.1772 + 0.4864 = 0.6636$ [Figure F3]

(iv) Between $Z = 0.81$ and $Z = 1.94$

Required area  
= (Area between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 1.94$)  
- (Area between $Z = 0$ and $Z = 0.81$)  
= $0.4738 - 0.2910 = 0.1828$ [Figure F4]

(v) To the left of $Z = -0.6$

Required area = (Area to left of $Z = 0$)  
- (Area between $Z = -0.6$ and $Z = 0$)  
= $0.5 - 0.2258 = 0.2742$ [Figure F5]
