**Example 12.7.**

The heights of freshmen students at a military academy are normally distributed with a mean of 5 feet 10 inches and a standard deviation of 2 inches.

(a) What is the proportion of freshmen at the academy who are taller than 6 feet 3 inches?
(b) What is the proportion of freshmen who are less than 5 feet 7 inches?
(c) What is the proportion of freshmen between 5 feet 8 inches and 6 feet 0 inches?

*Solution:* Here, $\mu = 70$, $\sigma = 2$ and $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} = \frac{X - 70}{2}$. Therefore

(a) $Z = \frac{75 - 70}{2} = 2.5$

$P( X > 75 ) = P( Z > 2.5 )$
$= P( 0 < Z < \infty ) - P( 0 < Z < 2.5 )$
$= 0.5 - 0.4938 = 0.0062$ or $0.62\%$

[Figure F2]

(b) $Z = \frac{67 - 70}{2} = -1.5$

$P( X < 67 ) = P( Z < -1.5 )$
$= P( -\infty < Z < 0 ) - P( -1.5 < Z < 0 )$
$= 0.5 - 0.4332 = 0.0668$ or $6.68\%$

[Figure F3]

(c) $Z_1 = \frac{X_1 - 70}{2} = \frac{68 - 70}{2} = -1$

$Z_2 = \frac{X_2 - 70}{2} = \frac{72 - 70}{2} = +1$

$P( 68 < X < 72 ) = P( -1 < Z < 1 )$
$= P( -1 < Z < 0 ) + P( 0 < Z < 1 )$
$= 0.3413 + 0.3413 = 0.6826$ or $68.26\%$

**Example 12.8.**

Suppose that the length of time it takes one variety of plant seeds to germinate is normally distributed with a mean of 15 days and a standard deviation of 4 days. What proportion of the seeds should germinate: (i) before 19 days? (ii) after 12 days? (iii) between 13 and 18 days?

*Solution:* Here, $\mu = 15$, $\sigma = 4$ and $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} = \frac{X - 15}{4}$. Therefore

(i). $Z = \frac{19 - 15}{4} = 1$

$P( X < 19 ) = P( Z \leq 1 )$
$= P( -\infty \leq Z \leq 0 ) + P( 0 \leq Z \leq 1 )$
$= 0.5 + 0.3413 = 0.8413$ or $84.13\%$
