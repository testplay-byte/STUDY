# Page 14 — Normal Distribution (Chapter 12)

> 📄 Original scan: [0014.jpg](../../../Raw/Statistics/Chapter-12-Normal-Distribution/0014.jpg) · printed page 136

$$\begin{aligned} Z_1 &= \frac{X_1 - 40}{4} \\ -2.33 &= \frac{X_1 - 40}{4} \\ X_1 - 40 &= 4(-2.33) \\ X_1 &= 40 - 9.32 = 30.68 \end{aligned} \qquad \begin{aligned} Z_2 &= \frac{X_2 - 40}{4} \\ +2.33 &= \frac{X_2 - 40}{4} \\ X_2 - 40 &= 4(2.33) \\ X_2 &= 40 + 9.32 = 49.32 \end{aligned}$$

Hence two points are 30.68 and 49.32.

(v) 95 % area under the normal curve means that 0.4750 area each to the left and right of mean. From the area table, the value of Z for which the area between 0 and Z is 0.4750 is 1.96. Since $X_1$ will be on the left of the mean, therefore $Z_1$ will be negative and $X_2$ is on the right of the mean, therefore $Z_2$ will be positive

[Figure F1]

$$\begin{aligned} Z_1 &= \frac{X_1 - 40}{4} \\ -1.96 &= \frac{X_1 - 40}{4} \\ X_1 - 40 &= 4(-1.96) \\ X_1 &= 40 - 7.84 = 32.16 \end{aligned} \qquad \begin{aligned} Z_2 &= \frac{X_2 - 40}{4} \\ +1.96 &= \frac{X_2 - 40}{4} \\ X_2 - 40 &= 4(1.96) \\ X_2 &= 40 + 7.84 = 47.84 \end{aligned}$$

Hence two points are 32.16 and 47.84.

(vi) P$_{20}$ is a point having 20 percent of the area below it. Area table shows this point to be

$$\begin{aligned} -0.84 &= \frac{X - 40}{4} \\ X - 40 &= 4(- 0.84) \\ X &= 40 - 3.36 = 36.64 \end{aligned}$$

Thus, $P_{20} = 36.64$.

[Figure F2]

P$_{80}$ is a point having 80 percent of the area below it. Area table shows this point to be

$$\begin{aligned} +0.84 &= \frac{X - 40}{4} \\ X - 40 &= 4(0.84) \\ X &= 40 + 3.36 = 43.36 \end{aligned}$$

Thus, $P_{80} = 43.36$.

[Figure F3]

P$_{95}$ is a point having 95 percent of the area below it. Area table shows this point to be

$$\begin{aligned} 1.645 &= \frac{X - 40}{4} \\ X - 40 &= 4(1.645) \\ X &= 40 + 6.58 = 46.58 \end{aligned}$$

Thus, $P_{95} = 46.58$.
