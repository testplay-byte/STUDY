## SHORT DEFINITIONS

**1. Normal Distribution**

A normal distribution is a particular idealized, smooth, bell-shaped histogram with all of the randomness removed. It represents an ideal data set that has lots of numbers concentrated in the middle of the range and trails off symmetrically on both sides. A data set follows a normal distribution if it resembles the smooth, symmetric, bell-shaped normal curve, except for some randomness. The normal distribution plays an important role in statistical theory and practice.

**2. Probability Density Function of Normal Distribution**

The probability density function for a normally distributed random variable X is

$$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2} \text{ for } -\infty < X < +\infty.$$

Where $\mu$ and $\sigma$ are any numbers such that $-\infty < \mu < +\infty$ and $\sigma > 0$. Where $e$ and $\pi$ are constants, $e = 2.7183$ and $\pi = 3.1416$.

**3. Normal Curve**

A normal curve is symmetrical and bell-shaped with its maximum height at the mean and the value of the maximum ordinate is $\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}$. The total area between the curve and score scale is unity and areas under portions of the curve may be treated as relative frequencies.

**4. Total Area Under the Normal Distribution Curve**

The total area under the curve for the normal distribution is unity, because the distribution is symmetric, the area under the curve to the left of the mean is 0.5 and the area under the curve to the right of the mean is 0.5. Whatever the value of mean and standard deviation for a normal distribution, the total area under the normal curve remains one. The normal distribution is not skewed; its skewness measure is zero.

**5. Importance of Normal Distribution**

The importance of the normal distribution is derived mainly from three essential considerations:

(i) Numerous variables seem to follow a pattern of variation that is similar to the normal distribution.

(ii) The sampling of many a sample statistic such as the mean has an approximately normal distribution.

(iii) The normal function is an excellent approximation to several other sampling distributions.

**6. Importance of Normal Distribution in Statistical Analysis**

Normal distribution is backbone of statistics because it is widely used in research in the biological, physical and social sciences. In practical life we quite often come across the distributions close to this distribution. Normal distribution is also called mother of distributions because various other distributions are generated from this distribution. Normal distribution makes the base for inferential statistics, a branch of statistics in which we draw conclusions about the population on the basis of sample information.

**7. Points of Inflection**

The inflection points of the curve occur one standard deviation on either side of the mean. Inflection points are points at which the bend in the curve changes direction.

**8. Shape of Normal Distribution**

Normal distribution has two parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma$. The shape of the normal distribution depends on the values of parameters. The location of a normal distribution is determined by the mean $\mu$ and the parameter $\sigma$ controls the relative flatness of the normal curve. If $\sigma$ is increasing the shape of the normal curve will be flatten. If $\sigma$ is decreasing the shape of the normal curve will be more peaked. If $\sigma$ is constant the shape of the normal curve will be remain same.
