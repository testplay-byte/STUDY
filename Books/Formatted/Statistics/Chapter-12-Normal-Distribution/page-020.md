## MULTIPLE - CHOICE QUESTIONS

1. The range of normal distribution is:
   - (a) $0$ to $n$
   - (b) $0$ to $\infty$
   - (c) $-1$ to $+1$
   - (d) $-\infty$ to $+\infty$

2. In a normal distribution 'X' lies between:
   - (a) $-\infty$ and $0$
   - (b) $-\infty$ and $+\infty$
   - (c) $0$ and $+\infty$
   - (d) $-1$ and $+1$

3. In normal distribution:
   - (a) mean = median = mode
   - (b) mean < median < mode
   - (c) mean > median > mode
   - (d) mean $\neq$ median $\neq$ mode

4. Which of the following is true for the normal curve?
   - (a) symmetrical
   - (b) unimodal
   - (c) bell-shaped
   - (d) all of the above

5. Mean and variance of a normal distribution are:
   - (a) n and p
   - (b) np and npq
   - (c) $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$
   - (d) $\pi$ and e

6. Normal distribution has $\underline{\hspace{2cm}}$ parameters.
   - (a) one
   - (b) two
   - (c) three
   - (d) four

7. In a normal distribution, P( $-\infty < X < +\infty$ ) is equal to:
   - (a) 0
   - (b) 0.5
   - (c) 1
   - (d) $\infty$

8. The shape of normal distribution is also called:
   - (a) leptokurtic
   - (b) mesokurtic
   - (c) platykurtic
   - (d) skewed

9. In a normal curve, the ordinate is highest at:
   - (a) mean
   - (b) variance
   - (c) standard deviation
   - (d) $Q_1$

10. The parameters of the normal distribution are:
    - (a) $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$
    - (b) $\mu$ and $\sigma$
    - (c) $\pi$ and e
    - (d) e and $\mu$.

11. The shape of the normal curve depends upon the value of :
    - (a) standard deviation
    - (b) $Q_1$
    - (c) mean deviation
    - (d) quartile deviation

12. The normal distribution is a proper probability distribution of a continuous random variable, the total area under the curve f( x ) is:
    - (a) equal to one
    - (b) less than one
    - (c) more than one
    - (d) between $-1$ and $+1$.

13. In a normal probability distribution of a continuous random variable, the value of standard deviation is:
    - (a) zero
    - (b) less than zero
    - (c) greater than zero
    - (d) none of the above

14. In a normal curve, the highest point on the curve occurs at the mean, $\mu$, which is also the:
    - (a) median and mode
    - (b) geometric mean and harmonic mean
    - (c) lower and upper quartiles
    - (d) variance and standard deviation

15. If $X \sim N$ ( 40, 25 ), the mean, median and mode are equal to:
    - (a) 5
    - (b) 25
    - (c) 40
    - (d) 65

16. In normal probability distribution for a continuous random variable, the value of mean deviation is approximately equal to :
    - (a) 2/3
    - (b) 2/3 $\sigma$
    - (c) 4/5
    - (d) 4/5 $\sigma$

17. In a normal distribution M.D.( X ) is equal to:
    - (a) 0.8989$\sigma$
    - (b) 0.7979$\sigma$
    - (c) 0.6969$\sigma$
    - (d) 0.5959$\sigma$

18. In a normal distribution whose mean is $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, the value of quartile deviation is approximately :
    - (a) 4/5
    - (b) 4/5 $\sigma$
    - (c) 2/3 $\sigma$
    - (d) 2/3

19. In a normal distribution, Q.D.( X ) is equal to:
    - (a) 0.6745
    - (b) 0.6745$\sigma$
    - (c) 0.7979$\sigma$
    - (d) 0.7979

20. In a normal distribution, the lower and upper quartiles are equidistant from the mean and are at a distance of :
    - (a) 0.7979
    - (b) 0.7979 $\sigma$
    - (c) 0.6745
    - (d) 0.6745 $\sigma$
