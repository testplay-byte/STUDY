**23.** The value of $\pi$ is approximately equal to:

**24.** The normal curve is symmetrical and for symmetrical distribution, the values of all odd order moments about mean will always be:
(a) 1 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.25 (d) 0

**25.** In normal distribution the values of moments ratios $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$ are respectively:
(a) 0 and 1 (b) $-1$ and $+1$ (c) 0 and 3 (d) 3 and 0

**26.** In normal distribution $\mu = 20$ and $\sigma^2 = 64$, the first mean moment is:
(a) 0 (b) 8 (c) 20 (d) 64

**27.** If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, the points of inflection of normal distribution are:
(a) $\pm \sigma$ (b) $\pm \mu$ (c) $\sigma \pm \mu$ (d) $\mu \pm \sigma$

**28.** The total area of the normal probability density function is equal to:
(a) 0 (b) 0.5 (c) 1 (d) 0.25

**29.** The normal probability density function curve is symmetrical about the mean, $\mu$, i.e. the area to the right of the mean is the same as the area to the left of the mean. This means that $P(X < \mu) = P(X > \mu)$ is equal to:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 0.5 (d) 0.25

**30.** The maximum ordinate of a normal curve is at:
(a) $X = \mu$ (b) $X = \mu + \sigma$ (c) $X = \mu - 2\sigma$ (d) $X = \sigma^2$

**31.** In the normal distribution, the value of the maximum ordinate is equal to:
(a) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$ (b) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi e}}$ (c) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi \sigma}}$ (d) $\frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}}$

**32.** The value of the ordinate at points of inflection of the normal curve is equal to:
(a) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$ (b) $\frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi e}}$ (c) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi \sigma}}$ (d) $\frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}}$

**33.** Which of the following is possible in normal distribution:
(a) $\sigma < 0$ (b) $\sigma = 0$ (c) $\sigma > 0$ (d) $\sigma > n$

**34.** The value of the standard deviation $\sigma$ of a normal distribution is always:
(a) equal to zero (b) greater than zero (c) less than zero (d) equal to 0.5

**35.** If X is a normal random variable having mean $\mu$, then $E|X - \mu|$ is equal to:
(a) variance (b) standard deviation (c) quartile deviation (d) mean deviation

**36.** If X is a normal random variable having mean $\mu$, then $E(X - \mu)^2$ is equal to:
(a) $\sigma^2$ (b) $\sigma$ (c) $3\sigma^4$ (d) $\beta_1$

**37.** The coefficient of skewness of a normal distribution is:
(a) positive (b) negative (c) zero (d) three

**38.** The skewness and kurtosis of the normal distribution are respectively:
(a) zero and zero (b) zero and one (c) one and zero (d) one and one

**39.** If $X \sim N (100, 64)$, then standard deviation $\sigma$ is:
(a) 100 (b) 64 (c) 8 (d) $100 - 64 = 36$

**40.** The value of second moment about the mean in a normal distribution is 5. The fourth moment about the mean in the distribution is:
(a) 5 (b) 15 (c) 25 (d) 75
