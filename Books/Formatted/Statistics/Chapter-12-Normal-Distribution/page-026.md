**Q.30** In a normal distribution the mean and standard deviation are 25 and 5 respectively. Find mean deviation and quartile deviation.

**Ans.** M.D. = 3.9895 and Q.D. = 3.3725

**Q.31** If $X \sim N(15, 4)$, the find the value of Z if $X = 18$.

**Ans.** 1.5.

**Q.32** Mean of normal distribution is 20. What will be the value of its mode?

**Ans.** 20

**Q.33** In a normal distribution, mean is zero and standard deviation is one. Find the value of the maximum ordinate correct to two places of decimal.

**Ans.** 0.40

**Q.34** If $X$ is $N(25,25)$. Find the value of the maximum ordinate.

**Ans.** 0.0798

**Q.35** In a normal distribution the lower and upper quartiles are 20 and 40 respectively. Find the parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma$.

**Ans.** 30, 14.83.

**Q.36** The value of second moment about the mean in a normal distribution is 4. Find $\mu_4$.

**Ans.** 48

**Q.37** In a normal distribution $\mu = 24$ and $\sigma = 4$. Find fourth moment about mean.

**Ans.** 768

**Q.38** In a normal distribution $\mu_4 = 243$. Find the parameter $\sigma$.

**Ans.** 3

**Q.39** In a normal distribution $\mu = 163$ and $Q_3 = 171.094$. Find the standard deviation.

**Ans.** 12

**Q.40** In a normal distribution the lower quartile is 10 and the standard deviation is 10. Find the mean, median and mode of the distribution.

**Ans.** Mean = Median = Mode = 16.745

**Q.41** A normal distribution has the mean $\mu = 85$, standard deviation $\sigma = 4.5$. Find the value of $Q_3$.
