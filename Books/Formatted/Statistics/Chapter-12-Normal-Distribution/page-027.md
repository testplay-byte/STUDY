**Q.53** For a normal distribution the first moment about 10 is 40 and the fourth moment about 50 is 48. Find mean and standard deviation of the distribution.

**Ans.** Mean = 50 and S.D. = 2.

**Q.54** If X is a normal random variable with $Q_1 = 200$ and $Q_3 = 253.96$, find $\beta_2$.

**Ans.** 3

**Q.55** In a normal distribution lower and upper quartiles are 20 and 44. Find the values of points of inflection in the normal distribution.

**Ans.** 14.21 and 49.79.

**Q.56** The value of fourth moment about the mean in a normal distribution is 1875. Find $\beta_2$.

**Ans.** 3

**Q.57** The fourth moment about mean of normal distribution is 768. Find mean deviation, quartile deviation, $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$.

**Ans.** M.D. = 3.1916, Q.D. = 2.698, $\beta_1=0$, $\beta_2=3$
