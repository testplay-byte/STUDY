**Q.83** If $X \sim N(100, 64)$, find the value of X which corresponds to a standardized value 2.

**Ans.** 116

**Q.84** If $X \sim N(\mu, 25)$ and $P(X \geq 30) = 0.0228$. Find $\mu$.

**Ans.** 20

**Q.85** If $X \sim N(69, \sigma^2)$ and $P(X < 72) = 0.8413$. Find $\sigma$.

**Ans.** 3

**Q.86** Sketch and verify the area under the normal curve between the mean and 0.44 standard deviations above and below it is 34%.

**Q.87** Sketch the normal curve and then place the values for the means on the respective X and Z scales. Verify that the area under the normal curve between the mean and 2.17 standard deviations above and below it is 0.97.

**Q.88** Sketch and verify the area under the normal curve between the mean and 3 standard deviations above and below it is 0.9973.

**Q.89** In a normal curve, verify that $\mu \pm 2\sigma$ covers 95.44% area.

**Q.90** In a normal curve, verify that $\mu \pm 1.96\,\sigma$ covers 95% area.

**Q.91** Show that $P(\mu - 1.5\sigma < X < \mu + 1.5\sigma) = 0.8664$.

**Q.92** What percentage of the area under the normal distribution curve falls within one standard deviation below and above the mean? Two standard deviations below and above the mean? Three standard deviations below and above the mean?

**Ans.** 34.13%, 95.45% and 99.73%.

**Q.93** Find the probability that the value of a standard normal variable exceeds 1.5.

**Ans.** 0.0668

**Q.94** Find the probability that the value of a standard normal variable is less than two.

**Ans.** 0.9772

**Q.95** If X is normally distributed with a mean of 4 and a standard deviation of 4, find the probability that X is less than 6.

**Ans.** 0.6915

**Q.96** The mean of a normal distribution is 400 pounds. The standard deviation is 10 pounds. What is the area between the mean and 395 pounds?

**Ans.** 0.1915 .

**Q.97** The mean wages of a certain group of workers working in a factory is Rs.285 with standard deviation of Rs.50. Find the percentage of workers who get above Rs.200.

**Ans.** 95.54%

**Q.98** The scores made by candidates in a certain test are normally distributed with mean equal to 500 and standard deviation equal to 100. Find the probability that a score will greater than 700.

**Ans.** 0.0228

**Q.99** Records from a dental practice show that the probability of waiting to go into the surgery for more than 20 minutes is 0.0228. If the waiting time is normally distributed with standard deviation 3.78 minutes, find the mean waiting time.

**Ans.** 12.44

**Q.100** A machine which automatically packs potatoes into bags is known to operate with a mean of 20 kg. and standard deviation of 0.5 kg. Assuming normality, find the percentage of bags weight more than 20 kg.

**Ans.** 50%

**Q.101** Guests at a large hotel stay for an average of 9 days with a standard deviation of 2.5 days. Among 1000 guests how many can be expected to stay less than 7 days. Assume that length of stay is normally distributed.

**Ans.** 212

**Q.102** The heights of a large sample of men were found to be approximately normally distributed with mean 67.56 inches and standard deviation 2.57 inches. Find the height exceeded by 5 % of the men.

**Ans.** 71.79

**Q.103** A manufacturer of pipe knows that the pipe lengths it produces vary in diameter and that the diameters are normally distributed. The mean diameter is 1 inch, and the probability that a length of pipe will have a diameter exceeding 1.1 inches is 0.1587. Find the variance of the diameters.

**Ans.** 0.01 (inches)$^{2}$
