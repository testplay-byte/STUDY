**Q.22** The probability of hitting a target in a shot is 3/5. If a person fires 20 shots at a target, let X denote the number of times he hits the target. By using the normal approximation to the binomial distribution, find the following probabilities:

(i) at least 6 shots hit the target (ii) at most 14 shots hit the target (iii) exactly 12 shots hit the target.

**Ans.** (i) 0.9985    (ii) 0.8729    (iii) 0.1820

**Q.23** A random variable X is normally distributed with mean = 70 and S.D. = 5.

(i) Find a point that has 87.9 % of the distribution below it.

(ii) Find a point that has 81.7 % of the distribution above it.

(iii) Find two such points between which the central 70% of the distribution lies

(iv) Find two such points between which the central 90% of the distribution lies

**Ans.** (i) 75.85    (ii) 65.48    (iii) 64.815, 75.185    (iv) 61.775, 78.225

**Q.24** Given a normal distribution with $\mu = 60$ and $\sigma = 5$.

(i) 14% of the values are less than what X value?

(ii) 97% of the values are between what two X values?

(iii) 94% of the values will be above what X value?

**Ans.** (i) 54.6    (ii) 49.15 and 70.85    (iii) 52.225

**Q.25** Given that $\mu = 300$ and $\sigma^2 = 100$. Find

(i) the area above 314    (ii) the two values that contain the middle 75 % area

(iii) $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ of the normal distribution.

**Ans.** (i) 0.0808    (ii) 288.5, 311.5    (iii) 293.255, 306.745

**Q.26** (i) The $90^{th}$ percentile of a normal distribution is 50 while the $15^{th}$ percentile is 25. Find $\mu$ and $\sigma$.

(ii) If $X \sim N(\mu, 144)$ and $P(X > 92) = 4.78\%$. Find the value of the mean $\mu$.

**Ans.** (i) $\mu = 36.20$ and $\sigma = 10.78$    (ii) $\mu = 72$ approximately

**Q.27** (a) If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $P(X < 35) = 0.20$ and $P(35 < X < 45) = 0.65$. Find mean and variance of the normal distribution.

(b) If X is normally distributed with parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$. Find the area under the curve between: (i) $(\mu - \sigma)$ and $(\mu + \sigma)$    (ii) $\mu$ and $(\mu + 2\sigma)$

**Ans.** (a) Mean = 39.47 and Variance = 28.3024    (b) (i) 0.6826    (ii) 0.4772

**Q.28** If $X \sim N(60, 100)$, find:

(i) a point that has 15 % area below it.    (ii) a point that has 28 % area above it.

**Ans.** (i) 49.6    (ii) 65.8

**Q.29** In a normal distribution $\mu = 40$ and $\sigma = 3.8$. Find:

(i) two points such that the curve has a 98 % chance of falling between them

(ii). the chance that a single observation will be less than 38.6.

**An** (i) 31.146, 48.854    (ii) 0.3557

**Q.30** If X is N( 24, 16 ), then find the: (i) 33rd percentile    (ii) 9th decile.

**Ans.** (i) 22.24    (ii) 29.12.

**Q.31** Given a normal distribution with $\mu = 40$ and $\sigma = 6$, find the value X that has:

(i) 38 % of the area below it.    (ii) 5% of the area above it.

**Ans.** (i) 38.167    (ii) 49.87
