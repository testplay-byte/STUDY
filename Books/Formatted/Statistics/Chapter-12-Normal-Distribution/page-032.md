**Q.32** In a normal distribution 30 % of the values are under 50 and 10 % are over 70. Find the mean and standard deviation of the distribution.

**Ans.** ( 55.81, 11.09 )

**Q.33** In a normal distribution 25 % of the items are under 60 and 15 % are over 90. Find the mean and standard deviation of the distribution.

**Ans.** ( 71.82, 17.53 )

**Q.34** In a normal distribution 7 % of the items are under 35 and 89 % are under 63. What is the mean and standard deviation of the distribution?

**Ans.** 50.29, 10.33

**Q.35** Assume that we have a large number of students whose average weight is 150 lbs. and that the weights are normally distributed. If we know that 36.4 % of the students have weight between 137 and 163 lbs. What is the standard deviation of the weights.

**Ans.** 27.66

**Q.36** (i) If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $P( X < 40 ) = 0.1587$ and $P( X > 70 ) = 0.0228$. Find $\mu$ and $\sigma$.  
(ii) If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $P( X > 30 ) = 0.0228$ and $P( X > 17.5 ) = 0.6915$. Find $\mu$ and $\sigma$.  
(iii) If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $P( X > 16 ) = 0.0668$ and $P( 8 < X < 12 ) = 0.3830$. Find $\mu$ and $\sigma$.  
(iv) If $X \sim N( 60, \sigma^2 )$ and $P( 50 \leq X \leq 70 ) = 0.8904$. Find $( 56 \leq X \leq 68 )$.

**Ans.** (i) 50, 10    (ii) 20, 5    (iii) 10, 4    (iv) 0.6386

**Q.37** In a "normal distribution" the lower and upper quartiles are 18 and 26 respectively. Find mean, standard deviation, mean deviation, quartile deviation, $\beta_1$, $\beta_2$ and value of maximum ordinate.

**Ans.** Mean = 22, S.D. = 5.93, M.D. = 4.73, Q.D. = 4, $\beta_1$ = 0, $\beta_2$ = 3 and Maximum ordinate = 0.0673.
