## MULTIPLE – CHOICE QUESTIONS

**1.** The collection of all individuals under consideration in a statistical study is called:

(a) sample (b) population (c) sampling (d) statistic

**2.** The set of all elements of interest in a particular study is called:

(a) population (b) sample (c) statistic (d) parameter

**3.** The whole material from which sample is drawn, is called:

(a) sampling unit (b) sample space (c) population (d) bias

**4.** Population size is denoted by:

(a) $n$ (b) $\sigma^2$ (c) $\mu$ (d) $N$

**5.** If all the units of a population are surveyed, it is called:

(a) random sample (b) random sampling (c) sampled population (d) complete enumeration

**6.** A population consisting of the items which are all present physically is called:

(a) finite population (b) infinite population (c) real population (d) hypothetical population

**7.** A population about which we want to get some information is called:

(a) finite population (b) infinite population (c) sampled population (d) target population

**8.** The population consists of the results of repeated trials is named as:

(a) finite population (b) infinite population (c) real population (d) hypothetical population

**9.** The part of population from which information is collected is called:

(a) census (b) parameter (c) sample (d) bias

**10.** Sample is a sub-set ( part ) of:

(a) population (b) data (c) set (d) distribution.

**11.** Number of observations falling in a sample are called:

(a) sample frame (b) sample design (c) population size (d) sample size

**12.** Any population constants is called a:

(a) statistic (b) parameter (c) estimate (d) estimator

**13.** A number that describes a population is called:

(a) sample (b) statistic (c) parameter (d) variable

**14.** Any measure or value calculated from population is called:

(a) finite (b) parameter (c) without replacement (d) random.

**15.** A numerical quantity which summarizes some characteristic of a sample is called:

(a) parameter (b) statistic (c) statistics (d) constant

**16.** Any measure or calculation on the sample data is called:

(a) parameter (b) statistic (c) $\bar{X}$ (d) error.

**17.** A number that describes a sample is called:

(a) population (b) parameter (c) statistic (d) estimation

**18.** If $N$ is the size of the population and $n$ is the size of the sample, then sampling fraction is:

(a) $n^N$ (b) $N^n$ (c) $n/N$ (d) $NC_n$

**19.** If size of the population is $N$ and size of the sample is $n$, the ratio $N/n$ is called:

(a) sampling fraction (b) sample proportion (c) sampling error (d) sampling interval

**20.** Study of population is called:

(a) parameter (b) statistic (c) error (d) census

**21.** A survey to collect data on the entire population is called:

(a) sample survey (b) census (c) sampling design (d) standard error

**22.** For making voters list in Pakistan we need:

(a) sampling error (b) standard error (c) census (d) simple random sampling

**23.** The collection of detailed information is known as:

(a) units (b) design (c) frame (d) census
