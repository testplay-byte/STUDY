**69.** A study conducted by a procedure that tends to overestimate or underestimate a population value is said to be:

(a) sampling error (b) random error (c) biased (d) standard error

**70.** A study conducted by a procedure that produces the correct result on the average is said to be:

(a) biased (b) unbiased (c) sample error (d) consistent

(a) unbiased sample variance (b) population variance

(a) unbiased sample variance (b) true variance

(a) probability sampling (b) simple random sampling
