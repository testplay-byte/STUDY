**Q.10.** A population consists of 4 elements 2, 4, 6, 8. Take all possible samples of size 2 with replacement and find their means. Make a sampling distribution of sample means and find its mean and variance. Also find the mean and variance of the population. Verify that:

(i) $\mu_{\bar{x}} = \mu$ (ii) $\sigma_{\bar{x}}^2 = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}$

**Ans:** $\mu_{\bar{x}} = 5, \sigma_{\bar{x}}^2 = 2.5, \mu = 5, \sigma^2 = 5$

**Q.11** What will be the mean and variance of sample means if (i) Sample of 36 is drawn with replacement from the population 1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 7. (ii) Sample of 4 is drawn without replacement from the population given in (i).

**Ans.** (i) $\mu_{\bar{x}} = 4.2$, Var ( $\bar{X}$ ) = 0.088 (ii) $\mu_{\bar{x}} = 4.2$, Var($\bar{X}$) = 0.527

**Q.12** (i) Given the following population distribution:

| X | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| f( X ) | 1/10 | 3/10 | 4/10 | 2/10 |

Find population mean and variance.

(ii) Given the following population distribution:

| X | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| f( X ) | 1/9 | 2/9 | 3/9 | 2/9 | 1/9 |

Calculate the population mean and standard deviation.

**Ans.** (i) 5.4 and 3.24 (ii) 4 and 1.1547

**Q.13** (i) A population consists of values 4,6,8,12,18 and .20. If samples of size 2 are drawn without replacement, then Compute $\mu_{\bar{x}}$ and $\sigma_{\bar{x}}^2$ without drawing samples.

(ii) If the mean and variance of a population are 20 and 4 respectively, what would be the mean and S.E($\bar{X}$) if the samples are drawn with replacement of size 5.

**Ans.** (i) 11.3333 and 14.22224 (ii) 20 and 0.8944

**Q.14** (i) If mean and variance of a population are 5 and 2.15 respectively. What would be the standard error of mean if samples of size 4 are drawn with replacement.

(ii) If mean and variance of population are 7 and 3.15 respectively. What would be standard error of mean if samples are drawn without replacement of size 6 from a population of size 10.

(iii) If the size of simple random sample is 49 and variance of sample means is 27. What must be the standard error of sample mean if n = 169.

**Ans.** (i) 0.7331 (ii) 0.4830 (iii) 2.80

**Q.15** (i) Standard error of $\bar{X}$ was computed as 2 for a sample of size 2 from the finite population of size 10 with variance $\sigma^2$. Find S.E($\bar{X}$) from the same population when sampling is done without replacement and n = 4.

(ii) If the size of the simple random sample from an infinite population is 25 and the standard error of the mean is 4. What must be the sample size if the standard error is reduced to 2?

(iii) If the size of the simple random sample from an infinite population is 60 and the variance of sample mean is 30. What must be the standard error of sample mean if n = 200?

**Ans.** (i) 1.2247 (ii) 100 (iii) 3

**Q.16** Assume that the masses of 2000 male students at a college are normally distributed with mean 65.0 kg and standard deviation 3.0 kg. If 100 samples consisting of 36 students each are obtained, what would be the expected mean and standard deviation of the resulting sampling distribution of means if sampling were done (i) with replacement (ii) without replacement?

**Ans.** (i) $\mu_{\bar{x}} = 65, \sigma_{\bar{x}} = 0.5$ (ii) $\mu_{\bar{x}} = 65, \sigma_{\bar{x}} = 0.5$
