**Q.26** Let $\bar{X}_1$ represent the mean of a random sample of size $n_1 = 2$ with replacement from a finite population consisting of values 4, 8. Similarly, let $\bar{X}_2$ represent the mean of a random sample of size $n_2 = 2$ with replacement from another finite population consisting of values 2, 4. Form a sampling distribution of $\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2$. Verify that: (i) $E(\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = \mu_1 - \mu_2$ (ii) $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = \frac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}$

**Ans.** $E(\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = 3$, $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = 2.5$, $\mu_1 = 6$, $\sigma_1^2= 4$, $\mu_2 = 3$, $\sigma_2^2= 1$

**Q.27** Draw all possible random samples of size $n_1 = 2$ without replacement from a finite population consisting of 3, 6, 9. Similarly draw all possible random samples of size $n_2 = 2$ without replacement from another finite population consisting of 2, 4, 6.

(a) Find the possible differences between the sample means of the two populations.

(b) Construct the sampling distribution of $\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2$ and compute its mean and variance.

(c) Verify that: (i) $\mu_{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2} = \mu_1 - \mu_2$ (ii) $\sigma_{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2}^2 = \frac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}\left(\frac{N_1-n_1}{N_1-1}\right) + \frac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}\left(\frac{N_2-n_2}{N_2-1}\right)$

**Ans:** $\mu_{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2} = 2$, $\sigma_{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2}^2 = 2.1667$, $\mu_1 = 6$, $\sigma_1^2= 6$, $\mu_2 = 4$, $\sigma_2^2= \frac{8}{3}$

**Q.28** Given $\mu_1 = 4500$, $\mu_2 = 4000$, $\sigma_1 = 200$, $\sigma_2 = 250$, $N_1 = 400$, $N_2 = 300$, $n_1 = 100$ and $n_2 = 50$. Determine the expected mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of difference of the means if sampling is done: (i) with replacement (ii) without replacement

**Ans:** (i) 500 and 40.62 (ii) 500 and 36.69

**Q.29** Given $N_1 = 125$, $n_1 = 30$, $\mu_1 = 78$, $\sigma_1^2 = 150$, $N_2 = 200$, $n_2 = 50$, $\mu_2 = 85$ and $\sigma_2^2 = 200$. Compute $E(\bar{X}_2 - \bar{X}_1)$ and $\text{Var}(\bar{X}_2 - \bar{X}_1)$ when sampling is done (i) with replacement (ii) without replacement

**Ans:** (i) 7 and 9 (ii) 7 and 6.85

**Q.30** (i) Given $N_1 = 125$, $n_1 = 30$, $\sigma_1^2 = 150$, $N_2 = 200$, $n_2 = 50$ and $\sigma_2^2 = 200$. Find $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_2 - \bar{X}_1)$ when sampling is done without replacement.

(ii) Given $n_1 = 24$, $\sigma_1 = 12$, $n_2 = 27$, $\sigma_2 = 18$. Find $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_2 - \bar{X}_1)$

**Ans:** (i) 6.8457 (ii) 18

**Q.31** The electric light bulbs of manufacturer A have a mean lifetime of 1200 hours with a standard deviation of 100 hours, while those of manufacturer B have a mean lifetime of 1400 hours with a standard deviation of 200 hours. If random samples of 125 bulbs of each brand are tested from two groups of the population 825 each. Compute the mean and variance of the sampling distribution of the difference $\bar{X}_2 - \bar{X}_1$ if sampling is done:

(i) with replacement (ii) without replacement.

**Ans:** (i) ( 200, 400 ) (ii) ( 200, 339.8 )

**Q.32** (i) Given $N_1 = 25$, $N_2 = 25$, $n_1 = 4$, $n_2 = 4$, $\sigma_1 = 4$ and $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = 10$. Find $\sigma_2$ when sampling is done without replacement.

(ii) Given $n_1 = 4$, $n_2 = 5$, $\sigma_2 = 5$ and $\text{Var} (\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2) = 14$. Find $\sigma_1$.

**Ans:** (i) 5.45 (ii) 6
