# Page 33 — Statistical Inference Estimation (Chapter 14)

> 📄 Original scan: [0033.jpg](../../../Raw/Statistics/Chapter-14-Statistical-Inference-Estimation/0033.jpg) · printed page 235

**Q.13** A random sample of 9 cigarettes of a certain brand has an average nicotine content of 3.6 milligrams and a standard deviation of 0.9 milligrams. Construct a 95 % confidence interval for the true average nicotine content of this particular brand of cigarettes, assuming an approximate normal distribution.

**Ans.** $2.91 < \mu < 4.29$

**Q.14** A certain machine is used to produce items whose weights are assumed to be normally distributed. Suppose that the variability in the weight of the output of the machine is unknown.

For a random sample of size 16, $\bar{X}$ is found to be 282 grams and unbiased standard deviation $s$ is 8 grams. Find a 95 % confidence interval for the true population mean.

**Ans.** $277.738 < \mu < 286.262$

**Q.15** A random sample of 12 observations which is selected from a normal population gave the results $\sum X = 1422$ and $\sum X^2 = 169350$. Construct the 99% confidence interval for $\mu$ and find its limits.

**Ans.** $110.65 < \mu < 126.35$

**Q.16** Find: (i) Point estimate and

(ii) 95% confidence interval for $\mu$ from the following sample 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17 and 19.

**Ans.** (i) 12 (ii) $7.90 < \mu < 16.10$

**Q.17** A sample of 15 values gives mean of 150 and S.D = 25. Find 98% confidence interval for population mean.

**Ans.** $133.06 < \mu < 166.94$

**Q.18** A firm wants to estimate the mean lifetime of a particular kind of tool. From the previous experience, it is known that the lifetimes are normally distributed. It draws a random sample of four of these tools and finds that their lifetimes are 7.9, 9.3, 10.8 and 11.4 years. Calculate a 95% confidence interval for the mean lifetime of this kind of tool.

**Ans.** $7.35 < \mu < 12.35$

**Q.19** The height in inches of 8 students of a college chosen at random were as follows: 62, 63, 71, 68, 65, 67, 61, 67. Compute a 98% confidence interval for the mean of the population of heights of the students of the college, assuming it to be normal.

**Ans.** $61.92 < \mu < 69.08$

**Q.20** A random sample of size $n_1 = 36$ taken from a normal population with a variance $\sigma_1^2 = 9$, has a mean $\bar{X}_1 = 75$. A second random sample of size $n_2 = 25$, taken from a different normal population with a variance $\sigma_2^2 = 25$, has a mean $\bar{X}_2 = 70$. Find a 98 % confidence interval for $\mu_1 - \mu_2$.

**Ans.** $2.4 < \mu_1 - \mu_2 < 7.6$

**Q.21** Two independent samples of 100 machinists and 100 carpenters are taken to estimate the difference between the hourly wages of the two categories of workers. The relevant data are given below:

| | Sample mean wage | Population variance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Machinists | 345 | 196 |
| Carpenters | 340 | 204 |

Determine the 95 % and 99 % confidence limits for the true difference between the average wages for machinists and carpenters. Which interval is wider?

**Ans.** 95 % confidence interval: $1.08 < \mu_1 - \mu_2 < 8.92$ and 99 % confidence interval: $- 0.15 < \mu_1 - \mu_2 < 10.15$. Therefore 99% interval is wider.
