**Example 15.6.**

A home heating oil delivery company would like to estimate the annual usage for its customers who live in single-family homes. A sample of 100 customers indicated an average annual usage of 1103 gallons and a sample standard deviation of 327.8 gallons. At the 1 % level of significance, is there evidence that the average annual usage exceeds 1000 gallons per year?

**Solution:**
(i) Null hypothesis: $H_0 : \mu \leq 1000$ . and Alternative hypothesis: $H_1 : \mu > 1000$
(ii) Level of significance: $\alpha = 0.01$

(iii) Test - statistic: $$Z = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{S / \sqrt{n}}$$

(iv) Critical region: $Z > 2.326$

( From the area table of normal distribution, we have $Z_{\alpha} = Z_{0.01} = 2.326$ )

(v) Computations: Here, $n = 100$, $\bar{X} = 1103$, $S = 327.8$, and hence

$$Z = \frac{1103 - 1000}{327.8 / \sqrt{100}} = \frac{103}{327.8} (10) = 3.142$$

(vi) Conclusion: Since the calculated value of Z = 3.142 falls in the critical region, so we reject our null hypothesis $H_0$: $\mu \leq 1000$ at 1 % level of significance and we may conclude that the average annual usage exceeds 1000 gallons per year.

**Example 15.7.**

A sample of 42 measurements was taken in order to test the null hypothesis that the population mean equals 8.5 against the alternative that it is different from 8.5. The sample mean and standard deviation were found to be 8.79 and 1.27, respectively. Perform the hypothesis test using 0.01 as the level of significance.

**Solution:**
(i) Null hypothesis: $H_0 : \mu = 8.5$ and Alternative hypothesis: $H_1 : \mu \neq 8.5$
(ii) Level of significance: $\alpha = 0.01$

(iii) Test - statistic: $$Z = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{S / \sqrt{n}}$$

(iv) Critical region: $\mid Z \mid > 2.575$ ( $Z < -2.575$ and $Z > 2.575$ )

( From the area table of normal distribution, we have $Z_{\frac{\alpha}{2}} = Z_{0.005} = 2.575$ )

(v) Computations: Here, $n = 42$, $\bar{X} = 8.79$, $S = 1.27$, and hence

$$Z = \frac{8.79 - 8.5}{1.27 / \sqrt{42}} = \frac{0.29}{1.27} \sqrt{42} = 1.48$$

(vi) Conclusion: Since the calculated value of Z = 1.48 falls in the acceptance region, so we accept our null hypothesis $H_0$: $\mu = 8.5$ at 1 % level of significance.
