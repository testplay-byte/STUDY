**Example 15.11.**

A random sample of 25 hens from a normal population showed that the average laying is 272 eggs per year with a variance of 625 eggs. The company claimed that the average laying is at least 285 eggs per year. Test the claim of the company at $\alpha = 0.05$.

**Solution:**

(i) Null hypothesis: $H_0$: $\mu \geq 285$ and Alternative hypothesis: $H_1$: $\mu < 285$

(ii) Level of significance: $\alpha = 0.05$

(iii) Test-statistic: $t = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{s / \sqrt{n}}$

(iv) Critical region: $t < -1.711$

(From the t-table, we have $-t_{\alpha(n-1)} = -t_{0.05(24)} = -1.711$)

(v) Computations: Here, n = 25, $\bar{X} = 272$, $s^2 = 625$, s = 25 and hence

$$t = \frac{272 - 285}{25 / \sqrt{25}} = \frac{-13}{5} = -2.6$$

(vi) Conclusion: Since the calculated value of t = – 2.6 falls in the critical region, so we reject our null hypothesis $H_0 : \mu \geq 285$ at 5% level of significance. On the basis of the evidence, we may conclude that the company's claim is not correct.

**Example 15.12.**

A sample of 12 jars of butter was taken from a lot, each jar being labelled 8 ounces net weight. The individual weights in ounces are: 8.2, 8.0, 7.6, 7.6, 7.7, 7.5, 7.3, 7.4, 7.5, 8.0, 7.4, and 7.5. Test whether these values are consistent with a population mean of 8 ounces. Assume that the weights are normally distributed. Use $\alpha = 0.05$.

**Solution:**

(i) Null hypothesis: $H_0 : \mu = 8$ and Alternative hypothesis: $H_1 : \mu \neq 8$

(ii) Level of significance: $\alpha = 0.05$

(iii) Test - statistic: $t = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{s / \sqrt{n}}$

(iv) Critical region: $|t| > 2.201$ ($t < -2.201$ and $t > 2.201$)

(From the t-table, we have $t_{\frac{\alpha}{2}(n-1)} = t_{0.025(11)} = 2.201$)

(v) Computations: Here, n = 12, $\sum X = 91.7$, $\sum X^2 = 701.61$, $\bar{X} = \frac{\sum X}{n} = \frac{91.7}{12} = 7.64$,

$$s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\left[\sum X^2 - \frac{(\sum X)^2}{n}\right] = \frac{1}{12-1}\left[701.61 - \frac{(91.7)^2}{12}\right] = 0.0790, \text{ s } = 0.28 \text{ and hence }$$

$$t = \frac{7.64 - 8}{0.28 / \sqrt{12}} = -\frac{0.36}{0.28} \sqrt{12} = -4.454$$

(vi) Conclusion: Since the calculated value of t = – 4.454 falls in the critical region, so we reject our null hypothesis $H_0$: $\mu = 8$ at 5 % level of significance. We may conclude that the values are not consistent with a population mean of 8 ounces.
