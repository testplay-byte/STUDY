**Q.28** State the appropriate null and alternative hypothesis from the following statements.
(i) Last month, the mean amount spent by customers at a certain restaurant was Rs. 2000. The restaurant owner believes that the mean may be higher this month.
(ii) Bags of a certain kind of rice are labeled as containing 5 kilograms. An inspector thinks that the mean weight may be less than this.
(iii) Last year, the mean monthly house rent in a certain city was Rs. 35000. A real estate agent believes that the mean rent is higher this year.
(iv) The mean weight of newborn girls in a certain town was 7 pounds. A doctor wants to know whether the mean weight of newborn girls differs from this.
(v) A research organization reports that 9% of all grocery shoppers never buy the store brand.
(vi) The average cost of a LED TV is not more than Rs. 40000.

**Ans:** (i) $H_0 : \mu = 2000$ and $H_1 : \mu > 2000$
(Right-tailed )
(ii) $H_0 : \mu = 5$ and $H_1 : \mu < 5$
(Left-tailed )
(iii) $H_0 : \mu = 35000$ and $H_1 : \mu > 35000$
(Right-tailed )
(iv) $H_0 : \mu = 7$ and $H_1 : \mu \neq 7$
(Two-tailed )
(v) $H_0 : p = 0.09$ and $H_1 : p \neq 0.09$
(Two-tailed )
(vi) $H_0 : \mu = 40000$ and $H_1 : \mu > 40000$
(Right-tailed )

**Q.29** Determine whether the alternative hypothesis is two-tailed, right-tailed or left-tailed.
(i) $H_0 : \mu = 100$ and $H_1 : \mu > 100$
(ii) $H_0 : \mu = 25$ and $H_1 : \mu < 25$
(iii) $H_0 : \mu = 38$ and $H_1 : \mu \neq 38$.

**Ans:** (i) Right-tailed (ii) Left-tailed
(iii) Two-tailed
