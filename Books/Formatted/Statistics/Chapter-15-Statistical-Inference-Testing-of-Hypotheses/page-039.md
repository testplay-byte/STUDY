**Q.40** Given $\sigma = 80$, $n = 625$, $\mu_0 = 350$ and $\bar{X} = 356$. Find Z.

**Ans.** 1.875

**Q.41** Given $\bar{X} = 100$, $\sigma_{\bar{x}} = 16$ and $\mu_0 = 90$. Find Z.

**Ans.** 0.625

**Q.42** Given $\bar{X} = 28$ and $\mu_0 = 28$. Find the value of Z-score.

**Ans.** $Z = 0$

**Q.43** Given $H_0$: $\mu = 12$, $H_1$: $\mu > 12$, $n = 64$, $\bar{X} = 15$, $\sigma = 10$ and $\alpha = 0.05$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 2.4$, reject $H_0$

**Q.44** Given $\bar{X} = 43260$, $H_0:\mu \leq 42000$, $H_1:\mu > 42000$, $\sigma = 5230$, $\alpha = 0.05$ and $n = 30$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 1.32$, Accept $H_0$

**Q.45** Explain the general procedure for testing of hypothesis regarding the . population mean when population standard deviation is unknown and the sample size is large.

**Q.46** Given $H_0$: $\mu = 150$, $H_1$ : $\mu \neq 150$, $n = 36$, $\bar{X} = 160$, $S = 60$ and $\alpha = 0.05$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 1$, accept $H_0$

**Q.47** Given $H_0$: $\mu = 100$, $H_1$: $\mu > 100$, $n = 49$, $\bar{X} = 105$, $S = 14$ and $\alpha = 0.01$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 2.5$, reject $H_0$

**Q.48** Describe the procedure for testing hypothesis about mean of a normal population when population standard deviation is unknown and the sample size is small.

**Q.49** Given $\bar{X} = 120$, $\mu_0 = 100$, $s = 34.75$ and $n = 25$. Find t.

**Ans.** 2.88

**Q.50** Given $\mu = 5$, $n = 9$, $\bar{X} = 2$ and $t = -2$. Find the value of s.

**Ans.** 4.5

**Q.51** Given $H_0$: $\mu=\mu_o$, $H_1$: $\mu \neq \mu_o$, $\alpha=0.05$, $t= -2.08$ and $n = 26$. Make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** reject $H_0$ and assert $H_1$

**Q.52** Given $H_0$: $\mu = 10$, $H_1$: $\mu \neq 10$, $n = 16$, $\bar{X} = 10.5$, $s = 0.75$ and $\alpha = 0.05$. Find t and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $t = 2.67$, reject $H_0$

**Q.53** Given $H_0$: $\mu = 10$, $H_1$: $\mu < 10$, $\alpha = 2.5\%$, $t = -2.08$ and $n = 22$. Make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** Accept $H_0$

**Q.54** Find the value of $t$ (test statistic) if $n = 20$, $\bar{X} = 4$, $s = 3$ and $\mu = 4$.

**Ans.** $t = 0$

**Q.56** Describe the procedure for testing equality of means of two normal populations when population standard deviations are known and sample sizes are large or small.

**Q.57** Given $\sigma_1^2 = 150$, $\sigma_2^2 = 180$, $n_1 = 30$ and $n_2 = 30$. Find $\sigma_{\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2}$.

**Ans.** 3.32

**Q.58** Given $H_0$: $\mu_1 = \mu_2$, $\bar{X}_1 = 6.53$, $\bar{X}_2 = 4.44$ and $\sigma_{\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2} = 0.78$. Find Z.

**Ans.** 2.68

**Q.59** Given $\bar{X}_1 = 26$, $\bar{X}_2 = 18$, $\sigma_{\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2} = 3.41$, $H_0$: $\mu_1 \leq \mu_2$ and $\alpha = 0.05$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 2.346$, reject $H_0$

**Q.60** Given $H_0$: $\mu_1 = \mu_2$, $H_1$: $\mu_1 \neq \mu_2$, $n_1 = 100$, $\bar{X}_1 = 14$, $\sigma_1^2 = 4$, $n_2 = 150$, $\bar{X}_2 = 11$, $\sigma_2^2 = 9$ and $\alpha = 5\%$. Find Z and make the statistical decision.

**Ans.** $Z = 9.49$, reject $H_0$
