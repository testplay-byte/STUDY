**Q.19** Given $n = 100$, $(A) = 40$. Find $(\alpha)$.
Ans. 60

**Q.20** Given $(AB) = 30$, $(A) = 40$. Find $(A\beta)$.
Ans. 10

**Q.21** Given $(A\beta) = 30$ and $(\alpha\beta) = 20$. Find $(\beta)$.
Ans. 50

**Q.22** Given $(B) = 50$ and $(AB) = 30$. Find $(\alpha B)$.
Ans. 20

**Q.23** Given $(\alpha BC) = 15$, $(\alpha B \gamma) = 60$, $(\alpha \beta C) = 40$ and $(\alpha \beta \gamma) = 70$. Find $(\alpha)$.
Ans. 185

**Q.24** Given $a = 55, b = 125, c = 45$ and $d = 75$. Find Chi-square ($\chi^2$).
Ans. 1.5625

**Q.25** Given $(A) = 20$, $(B) = 13/50$, $(AB) = 1/50$ and $n = 250$. Whether attributes A and B are negatively associated?
Ans. Yes, $(AB) < \frac{(A)(B)}{n}$

**Q.26** Given $(A) = 304$, $(B) = 1024$, $(AB) = 256$ and $n = 1216$. Show that attributes A and B are independent.
Ans. $(AB) = \frac{(A)(B)}{n} = 256$

**Q.27** Given $(A) = 34000$, $(B) = 6000$, $(AB) = 5300$ and $n = 70000$. Whether attributes A and B are positively associated?
Ans. Yes, $(AB) > \frac{(A)(B)}{n}$

**Q.28** If $(A) = 20$, $(B) = 10$ and $n = 40$. Find $(AB)$ if A and B are independent attributes.
Ans. 5

**Q.29** Given $n = 600$, $(A) = 240$ and $(B) = 270$. What would be the frequency of $(AB)$ if attributes A and B are independent?
Ans. 108

**Q.30** Determine whether two attributes are independent or associated.
$N = 1024$, $(A)=144$, $(B)=384$, $(AB)=54$.
Ans. Independent

**Q.31** Given $(AB) = 70$, $(\alpha \beta) = 140$, $(A\beta) = 10$ and $(\alpha B) = 30$. Whether two attributes A and B are independent, positively associated or negatively associated?
Ans. Positively associated.

**Q.32** Given the data for two attributes A and B as, $n=100$, $(\alpha \beta)=30$, $(B)=60$ and $(\alpha)=70$. Find other frequencies of a $2 \times 2$ contingency table.
Ans. $(AB) = 20$, $(\alpha B) = 40$, $(A\beta) = 10$, $(A) = 30$ and $(\beta) = 40$.

**Q.33** Given the data for two attributes A and B as, $(AB) = 20$, $(\alpha) = 65$, $(\beta) = 50$ and $(A) = 35$. Find other frequencies of a $2 \times 2$ contingency table.
Ans. $(\alpha B) = 30$, $(B) = 50$, $(A\beta) = 15$, $(\alpha \beta) = 35$ and $n = 100$.

**Q.34** Given $(A) = 100$, $(\alpha) = 40$, $(\beta) = 105$ and $(\alpha \beta) = 30$. Whether attributes A and B are independent?
Ans. Yes, attributes A and B are independent.

**Q.35** Given $(\alpha) = 45$, $(A\beta) = 40$, $(\alpha B) = 20$ and $n = 140$. Show that attributes A and B are positively associated.
Ans. Attributes A and B are positively associated.
