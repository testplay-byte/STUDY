## 1.9.4 Solving Problems Graphically from Daily Life

See the following example to understand the method.

**Example 17:** Two airplanes are moving along the paths representing $f(x) = x + 2$ and $g(x) = 2x - 4$ respectively. Draw the graph of paths of both planes and find the point, where both planes pass.

**Solution:**
In the figure, red line represents the path of first plane while blue line represents the path of second plane.
The graph shows that both planes pass through the point (6, 8).
