# Page 44 — Probability (Chapter 9) — Short Questions

> 📄 Original scan: [0044.jpg](../raw/S-2/0044.jpg) · printed page 54

**Q.48** If three coins are tossed, what is the probability of getting fewer than two heads?

**Ans.** 1/2

**Q.49** A coin is tossed three times and the outcome is recorded for each toss. Let A be the event that the experiment yields two tails. List the sample points in A.

**Ans.** $\{(H, T, T), (T, H, T), (T, T, H)\}$

**Q.50** If we roll a balanced die, what is the probability of getting an even number?

**Ans.** 1/2

**Q.51** Two dice are rolled. Count the total number of sample points in the sample space S.

**Ans.** 36

**Q.52** A pair of dice is thrown. What is the probability that the sum equals 2?

**Ans.** 1/36

**Q.53** A die is rolled two times. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers observed will be greater than 9?

**Ans.** 1/6

**Q.54** A pair of dice is rolled. What is the probability that the sum equals 7?

**Ans.** 1/6

**Q.55** Suppose two balanced dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum of the dice is even?

**Ans.** 1/2

**Q.56** What is the probability of rolling a eleven with a pair of balanced dice?

**Ans.** 1/18

**Q.57** When a card is picked from a well-shuffled deck of 52 playing cards, what is the probability that it will be a black ace?

**Ans.** 1/26

**Q.58** If a card is chosen from a well-shuffled deck of 52 playing cards, what is the probability of selecting a 10 of any suit?

**Ans.** 1/13

**Q.59** A card is selected from a deck of playing cards. What is the probability that the card is a king or queen?

**Ans.** 2/13

**Q.60** A card is selected from a deck of playing cards. What is the probability that the card is a red or black?

**Ans.** 1

**Q.61** A card is drawn at random from a deck of 52 playing cards. What is the probability that it will be an ace or a face card?

**Ans.** 4/13

**Q.62** A card is drawn at random from a deck of 52 playing cards. What is the probability that it will be a spade or a face card?

**Ans.** 11/26

**Q.63** Two cards are drawn at random from an ordinary deck of 52 cards. Determine the probability that both cards are aces, if the first card is replaced before the second card is drawn.

**Ans.** 1/169

**Q.64** If A and B are mutually exclusive events and P( A ) = 0.4, P( B ) = 0.5 then, find P( A ∪ B ) also find $P(\bar{A})$ and $P(\bar{B})$.

**Ans.** 0.9, 0.6 and 0.5

**Q.65** Given P( A ) = 0.6 and P( B ) = 0.3. If A and B are mutually exclusive events, then find P( A ∩ B ).

**Ans.** $P(A \cap B) = 0$

**Q.66** If P( A ) = 0.3, P( B ) = 0.8 and P( A ∩ B ) = 0.2. Find P( A ∪ B ).

**Ans.** 0.9

**Q.67** Suppose P(A) = 1/3, P( A ∪ B ) = 1/2 and P( A ∩ B ) = 1/10. Find P( B ).

**Ans.** 0.27

**Q.68** Suppose P( A ) = 1/4, P( B ) = 1/3 and P( A ∪ B ) = 1/2. Determine P( A ∩ B ).

**Ans.** 1/12

**Q.69** Given P( A ) = 1/3 and P( B ) = 1/4. Suppose A and B are independent events, determine P( A ∪ B ).

**Ans.** 1/2

**Q.70** Given P( A ) = 0.60 and P( B ) = 0.10. If A and B are not mutually exclusive events, then find P( A ∪ B ).

**Ans.** 0.64
