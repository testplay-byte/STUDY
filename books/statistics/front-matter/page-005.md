# Page 5 — Basic Statistics Part-II: Preface (continuation)

> 📄 Original scan: [0005.jpg](../raw/S-0/0005.jpg) · printed page ( iv )

**Long-Questions:** The fourth merit this book is embellished with are solved long questions both from exercises as well as from the past papers. This section contains a variety of questions concerning almost every field of life. Having read and solved these questions, it is expected that reader will not encounter any type of difficulty in the use of Statistics under any circumstances that of examination or practical life.

**Solved Examples:** The fifth quality in this book are solved examples. These examples have been solved in line with solution of each topic discussed in theory and formulas. Repetition of these examples has been avoided to all possible extents except where the repetition was absolutely unavoidable. These examples have been solved in finger-lead guideline manner.

> *Last but not the least, the answer of each question is knit together as to help students to ensure the accuracy and certainty of their answers. The students will not astray from pillar to post for the certainty and accuracy of their answers. Having completed these abovementioned five portions, students will be able to grasp the respective topic more than 95%. Even then if the students feel any deficiency, the theoretical portion of this book will help them overcome those deficiencies.*

If you need to know how to use a wide range basic and comprehensive knowledge of statistical techniques without getting in over your head, this is the book you want.

This book has been an outcome of successive years of my teaching experience in various prestigious institutions at MSc Statistics and BS Statistics level and other disciplines with a complete awareness of the needs of the students, demands of the subject and requirements of modern, rapidly changing and up-to-dated examination system.

As knowledge is shared property of all and alike and no one's individual possession, I don't dare claim 100% originality of the work. I have freely consulted hundreds of books written by foreign authors and I am immensely grateful to all of them whose names cannot be mentioned individually in this nature of work

This 6<sup>th</sup> Edition of this book is an outcome of successive years of unending toil. During the course from 1<sup>st</sup> Edition till the recent most 6<sup>th</sup> edition, I have been approached and I myself met with hundreds of erudite teachers and passionate students of this field as well as many individuals from various walks of life in general. Based on the feed-back, suggestions, appreciations and criticism of worthy teachers from different Universities and colleges, and demands of the students and needs of the examination system, I have made a number of amendments in all the editions of this book over the years. And now I can humbly claim that there is sky-line difference in the 1<sup>st</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> edition of this book. This can now be rated as a good book.

Lots of cares and pains have been taken while writing and proof reading this book yet I don't claim the book to be error-free. There may be erratum's which will be earnestly welcomed for rectifications from the revered teachers and dear students. Suggestions and constructive criticism for the improvement of this book and enhancement of the knowledge will be highly appreciated from amigos of the teaching community.

I am immensely gratified to my family, friends, and colleagues from the teaching community who have invigorated me to use my possible potentials for producing this book to make my experience immortal in form of this book for the welfare of the posterity generations.
