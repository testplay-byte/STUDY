## Preface

As Statistics has been a very practical, widely used and perplexing subject, difficulties in the subject also enhance with advancements in the level of the students. Despite the fact th.t students get introduced with the subject at the initial level of Part-I, yet it .becomes so difficult for them to handle the texts of foreign writers, increasing level of difficulties and wide range of knowledge. Basic Statistics Part-II is specifically designed to address the above mentioned predicaments of the students.

You get a firm hands-on advanced understanding of how to apply them before moving on to the more advanced material that follows. The $1^{\text{st}}$ edition of this book was not sufficient for the needs of the students for examination at that time. And the no single book was sufficient for .attempting the entire paper as the syllabi's and paper patterns of all boards were being changed rapidly and even one different from that of the other. The need for such a book which would fulfill all the needs of the paper attempt was direly felt. After an unending struggle of consecutive years of hard work knit together with industrious and laborious nature re-writing, re-compiling, re-editing and huge expenditure were so tiring and torturing. During the course from $1^{\text{st}}$ to the $6^{\text{th}}$ edition, it can now be said with certainty that this $6^{\text{th}}$ edition now mitigates all needs of the examinations, syllabus, students and field of knowledge. This edition of *Basic Statistics Part-II* has been amended after a thorough examination of past papers of all the educational boards of *Punjab* including *FBISE* and educational boards of *AJK* keeping in mind the nature of questions in all parts of the paper of the respective boards. This edition of *Basic Statistics Part-II* tends to satiate all the apprehensions of the pupils regarding examination and will help them exactly comprehend the technique which help them secure maximum grades with the qualitative, up-to-date and to-the-point material available in $6^{\text{th}}$ edition of *Basic Statistics Part-II*. This book assuages the needs of Intermediate students in particular but at the same time, this book can be useful for the students of B. Com, M.A Economics, MSc Psychology, MSc Geography Business Administration, or other areas of social sciences. This book also covers many papers of BS 4 years' program. The book is very handy for minor courses of Statistics being taught in different BS disciplines. This is an all in one package for the students.

Organized into six distinct sections, *Basic Statistics Part-II* provides you with:

**Short Definitions:** The first thing that makes this book stand out form rest of the books available in the market are short definitions. It is the distinction of this book that complex and confusing definitions of basic concepts have been gleaned in such a simple and effective way that even a man entirely unknown to Statistics can easily understand and learn them by heart. The definitions are brief yet comprehensive. It is claimed that these definitions cover almost the whole of the questions coming in examinations based on this section.

**Multiple Choice Questions:** The second characteristic that makes this book worth reading are the number of solved Multiple Choice Questions. The biggest distinction of these Multiple Choice Questions leis in there being able to cover almost every angle of not only the statement of the questions but also the wide variety of options written. Every angle has been kept in mind the paper, examinations, level of the students and requirement of the subject itself. These Multiple Choice Questions have been a montage of simplicity and effort. A part of Multiple Choice Questions has been solved in such a plain way that a single reading will enable students understand those Multiple Choice Questions. But in some Multiple Choice Questions, the students will have to put some effort in order to understand and analyses their answers.

**Short Questions:** The third trait that this book celebrates are the short questions. These short questions not only cover the complete range of possible short questions occurring in the examinations but also let the students relish the certainty of these questions to definitely come the examinations. These Short Questions are self-sufficient both in quality and quantity. It is inevitable for the paper-setter to ignore the importance of these short questions while setting the paper.
