**Example 26:** Draw the graph of $y = |x|$ and then sketch the graphs of:
(a) $y = |1.5x|$ and $y = |-1.5x|$ (b) $y = \frac{|x|}{1.5}$ (c) $y = \frac{|x|}{-1.5}$
