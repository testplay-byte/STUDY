**Q.9** Let $A = \{6, 7\}$, $B = \{7, 8\}$ and $C = \{8\}$ be subsets of the universal set $S = \{6, 7, 8\}$. Determine the elements of the following sets:

(i) $A \times C$  (ii) $A \times B$  (iii) $B \times A$  (iv) $(A \times B) \cup (A \times C)$  (v) $(A \times B) \cap (A \times C)$.

**Ans.** (i) $\{(6, 8), (7, 8)\}$ (ii) $\{(6, 7), (6, 8), (7, 7), (7, 8)\}$ (iii) $\{(7, 6), (7, 7), (8, 6), (8, 7)\}$

(iv) $\{(6, 7), (6, 8), (7, 7)(7, 8)\}$ (v) $\{(6, 8), (7, 8)\}$
